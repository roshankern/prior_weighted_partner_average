# 4 · PIE's benchmark and RFE selection

This stage asked two questions. First: how does PWPA (Prior-Weighted Partner Average) score on the benchmark of PIE
(Verma et al. 2026, code `ArcInstitute/pie` at commit `21a526a`), using PIE's own arrays, split and metric code? Second:
can a simpler dataset-selection step, RFE (recursive feature elimination), replace the L1 path and decoys used until
then? The work ran on 2026-10-06 (K001–K020, L005), 2026-10-07 (L007) and 2026-10-14 (K030–K042, R031–R033). Almost
all of it was done at the researcher's request, in interactive sessions or by subagents, outside the lab loop.
`findings.md` has no entry for any of these ids, so the experiment READMEs and result files are the only record.
Datasets: PIE's Replogle–Nadig Perturb-seq benchmark (K562, RPE1, HepG2, Jurkat), PIE's Jiang 2025 and Tahoe-100M data
(K040 only), the lab's Replogle K562 (`replogle_k562_expr`) and Marson CD4+ T cells (`marson_dev`). Two metric
families are in force. Never compare them on one scale:
- **PIE's metrics** (K010–K042), from PIE's unmodified code at FDR 0.05: AUPRC, Jaccard, DE direction match,
  Spearman effect size, Spearman LFC and Discrimination L1. The split is PIE's `unseen_pert` split with 4 folds, each
  fold holding out one cell line; the other three lines are scored. Values are mean ± **sample sd over the 4 folds**,
  as in PIE's Table 4, not SE. The one exception is K034, which reports SE.
- **The lab's metric** (R031–R033, L005, L007, plus a side table in K010/K011): a fixed panel of 300 genes ("panel
  300") with Pearson Δ − k and MSE in log2FC. Unless a row says otherwise, these are dev folds 0–3, and the lockbox
  (fold 4) is never read. The biased "own top-N genes" metric of sessions 1–4 is not used anywhere in this stage.

Terms used below:
- **Δ** is a knockdown's expression change from control.
- **k** is the context mean: the mean Δ of the training knockdowns in the same cell context.
- **b** is the baseline that predicts k for every knockdown. On Pearson Δ − k, b scores 0. On MSE, b is the bar to beat.
- **Pearson Δ − k** is the Pearson correlation between predicted and true Δ after subtracting k from both. It is
  computed over panel 300, the 300 genes with the largest mean |z| over the training knockdowns of that (condition,
  fold).
- **r** is the replicate agreement between two halves of the cells. It is the noise ceiling.
- **Gate:** the one fitted number per prior dataset. Weights are entmax-1.5 (a sparse softmax) over Σ gate · z, where
  z is that dataset's standardised gene–gene similarity.
- **Share** is gate / sum of gates.
- **Plain form:** a plain weighted average of training knockdowns. Rows of the weight matrix W sum to 1.
- **L1 path (H003):** gate fits along a sequence of L1 penalties.
- **Decoy (H003):** a copy of each source with gene identities shuffled.
- **Decoy-calibrated rule (H003b):** keep the sources whose gates beat their decoys at the chosen penalty.
- **Refit / "-cal" arm:** the kept set refitted without a penalty. This is the arm that is scored.
- **H013-plain / H013x-expr-plain:** the model of record before this stage (41 candidate sources, L1 path, decoys,
  refit). Its Marson dev score is Pearson Δ − k 0.2706 ± 0.0076.
- **J011:** the session-9 arm that ran this pipeline on the expanded pool of about 119 sources.
- **Rule B:** a pool rule that removes every Perturb-seq source from the dataset's own cell system.
- **Coverage rule:** drops sources that cover too few knockdowns.
- **coess:** DepMap co-essentiality with 5 PCs removed. **depmap:** raw DepMap gene-effect profiles.
- **string:** STRING v12 top-20 edges, the lab's own build. **Fawkes:** the STRING neighbour-average baseline.
- **Lockbox:** fold 4 of each lab dataset, held out from all development work.

## What this stage established

1. **The unchanged session-9 pipeline beats PIE's published row on 5 of 6 of PIE's own metrics** (K010, K011, K020).
   It used the L1 path, decoys and refit, with no tuning for this benchmark.
   - Extended pool (103 sources): AUPRC 0.343 ± 0.025 against PIE's 0.304 ± 0.023. Jaccard 0.203 against 0.168.
     Discrimination L1 0.746 against 0.709.
   - The pool with no measured transcriptional response to the test gene from any source (93 sources): AUPRC
     0.340 ± 0.024.
   - Spearman LFC is level: 0.546 and 0.544 against 0.552.
   - All values are the sd over 4 folds. PIE publishes no per-fold values, so no paired test exists.
2. **Scoring choices move PIE's metrics as much as the models do** (K020).
   - Ranking genes by their DE frequency over the training knockdowns, the same ranking for every test knockdown,
     scores AUPRC 0.282 ± 0.023. PIE's published Context-mean row scores 0.082. PIE's own ablation baseline with no
     sources scores 0.281 (K034).
   - With the on-target "fill", a model with no information about the knockdown scores AUPRC 0.409, above PIE's
     0.304. The fill ranks the knocked-down gene itself as DE. Headline numbers therefore have the fill off.
   - The honest floor for AUPRC is 0.282, not 0.082.
3. **With exactly PIE's five gene sources as priors, PWPA still scores above PIE on AUPRC: 0.332 against 0.304**
   (K030, K031).
   - It is above PIE on Jaccard (0.184) and Spearman effect size (0.593).
   - It is level on direction match (0.785) and Discrimination L1 (0.704 against 0.709).
   - It is below on Spearman LFC (0.525 against 0.552).
   - It is 0.008 AUPRC below the 93-source pool.
   - The clean repo reruns this with RFE in `2.train_test/0.pie_benchmark.ipynb`. That rerun replaces it as the PWPA
     bar of the main figure.
4. **Over the context mean, PWPA gains twice what PIE gains from the same five sources** (K034). The measure is the
   exact Shapley value on AUPRC, mean ± SE over the 4 folds.
   - Gain: +0.050 for PWPA, +0.025 for PIE.
   - Per source: STRING SPACE +0.019 ± 0.0005, DepMap +0.014 ± 0.0005, gene description text +0.009 ± 0.0004, NCBI
     annotations +0.008 ± 0.0005, ESM2 +0.0001 ± 0.0001.
   - Gate share ranks the sources differently (DepMap 34%, annotations 26%, STRING 21%). Gate size is not
     contribution.
5. **Perturb-seq from other cell systems adds little on PIE's split** (K010 against K011). The lab had predicted a
   gap of more than +0.03 panel Pearson on most datasets. That prediction was refuted:
   - The gap is +0.020 on average (−0.004 to +0.047), above 0.03 on only 4 of 12 datasets.
   - On AUPRC the gap is 0.343 against 0.340.
6. **RFE became the default selection on 2026-10-14. It matches or beats the L1 path + decoy rule with fewer
   datasets** (R031, R032). Scores are dev panel 300 Pearson Δ − k.
   - Replogle K562, 14 candidates: all 14 score 0.6147, and the RFE path keeps that score down to 7 datasets
     (paired 7 − 14: −0.0000 ± 0.0007). It falls at 4 (−0.018 ± 0.003) and at 1 (0.4859). For comparison: the
     original PWPA (41 candidates) 0.6000, the decoy rule on the same 14 candidates (R030) 0.6131, Fawkes 0.5210.
   - Marson, 15 candidates: all 15 score 0.2738 ± 0.0076. The kept size by the 1-SE rule is 7 datasets (0.2718).
     For comparison: L007 decoy rule 0.2731, model of record 0.2706, Fawkes 0.1661.
7. **The drop order is stable across folds and differs by dataset** (R031, R032, R033).
   - On K562, coess is last in every fold and GO BP is second-to-last. The T-cell screens leave first.
   - On Marson, DepMap is last in every fold, with k562 and coess next. GO, CORUM, Wang, ORCS_1110 and Legut leave
     first.
8. **Adding PIE's five sources to the K562 pool helps nothing measurable and makes the RFE path worse at small
   sizes** (R033). Scores are dev panel 300 Pearson Δ − k.
   - All 19 against all 14: +0.0021 ± 0.0014. At 7 datasets: −0.0100 ± 0.0032. At 5: −0.0125 ± 0.0033.
   - The cause: PIE's STRING SPACE and perturbation-text sources get large gates and outlast depmap and string,
     which carry more. Gate size ignores coverage and overlap. This is a known weakness of gate-based RFE.
9. **Failed idea: line priors for unseen cell contexts.** On PIE's unseen-context split (K040–K042), an equal-weight
   average of the same knockdown in the other lines (t0:tie) scores AUPRC 0.304 ± 0.017. PIE scores .424.
   - Learned line priors (gated2:tie, 0.289 ± 0.029) did not raise AUPRC.
   - Gates learned on DepMap/PRISM and applied frozen did not raise it either (0.302 ± 0.017).
   - The line priors explain only about 2% of cross-line response MSE.
10. **PWPA learns fast but has not saturated** (L005, L007). Both are Marson dev, panel 300.
    - One gradient step reaches Pearson Δ − k 0.217, against 0.273 after 1,000 steps.
    - Pearson rises from 0.047 ± 0.004 at 32 training knockdowns per state to 0.271 ± 0.008 at 664 and is still
      rising. This curve is flattened at small sizes by dataset selection on the full data.
    - The 15-dataset pool loses nothing against the 41-dataset pool: paired +0.0025 ± 0.0015. That pool became the
      candidate pool for RFE.

## Experiments

### K001 · PIE's arrays and split as 12 lab datasets
- **Question:** Can PIE's own preprocessed Replogle–Nadig arrays and its `unseen_pert` split go into the lab's
  `ext_score` format without changing a value, so that the unchanged pipeline trains and tests on exactly PIE's rows,
  genes and labels? Asked by the researcher for the PIE comparison (PIE Table 4: unseen perturbations in seen
  contexts).
- **Setup:** Inputs:
  - HF `arcinstitute/pie_replogle_nadig_essential` at commit `d4b7b6b…`, SHA-256 checked against PIE's `meta.json`.
  - PIE's split files at commit `396ab95…`.

  The output is 12 units, `pie_f<fold>_<line>`, where fold is the line PIE holds out and line is one of the other
  three. Each unit has:
  - 6,642 readout genes;
  - fold 1 = PIE's training knockdowns (784–923 per unit) and fold 0 = PIE's test knockdowns (367–452);
  - PIE's validation knockdowns in no set.

  Rule B is set so that all four lines' Perturb-seq sources leave every pool: 103 sources in "expanded", 40 in
  "current", the same list for all 12 units. Nothing is fitted.
- **Prediction:** none recorded.
- **Result:** Every check passed:
  - `delta_p` was copied bit for bit;
  - all 6,642 readout symbols mapped to ENSG (0 unmapped);
  - no test knockdown appears in any line's train or validation list;
  - no constant channel exists in any unit (smallest sd 0.018).

  Things a later reader must know:
  1. PIE ships no replicate halves, so **there is no r and no noise ceiling** on these datasets.
  2. PIE's knockdown lists overlap the lab's lockbox: 145–216 knockdowns per PIE dataset are fold 4 of the lab's
     dataset of the same line.
  3. In 4,103 of 5,459 rows, `delta_p` at the knocked-down gene is exactly minus the control mean.
- **Verdict:** infrastructure.
- **For later work:** Score these units with `--folds 0` only. Fold 1 as a test fold would train on PIE's test rows.
  A fit on these units sees lockbox responses through PIE's pseudobulks.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/K001-pie-prep/` (archived: no), no findings entry.

### K010 · Unchanged session-9 pipeline on PIE's split, extended pool (with K010-cal)
- **Question:** How does the unchanged H013-plain pipeline score on PIE's unseen-perturbation split, on PIE's own
  rows, genes and metrics? It was run as J011-expanded-path, then J011-expanded-path-cal.
- **Setup:** 12 PIE units, test fold = PIE's test knockdowns. The pool is the expanded pool: 103 sources minus rule B
  and the coverage rule. Kept from other cell systems: `ps:marson_*` (3), `ps:kolf_ipsc`, `ps:xatlas_hct116`,
  `ps:xatlas_hek293t` and three L1000 sources.

  Fit: L1 path with decoys (206 channels), H003b rule, unpenalised refit in the plain form, 5 seeds. The fitted
  numbers are one gate per kept source (10–18 sources kept per unit).

  `K010-pie-extended-path-cal` dumps P (the prediction) and W (the weights; P = k + W(X_train − k), rows sum to 1)
  for the scorer K020. Comparators: K011, and PIE's published Table 4.
- **Prediction:** Written before any job ran:
  - panel 300 Pearson Δ − k between 0.35 and 0.60 on each of the 12 datasets;
  - extended above matched (K011) by more than +0.03 on most datasets, because X-Atlas is still in this pool.
- **Result:** All 24 path jobs and 24 refit jobs completed. Every dump passed its self-check
  (max |k + W(X − k) − P| < 4e-6).

  **Lab metric**, panel 300 on PIE's test rows (lockbox knockdowns included, no r): Pearson Δ − k 0.500–0.575, mean
  0.539. STRING on the same rows scores 0.333–0.425. MSE is below both STRING and b on all 12 units; for example,
  `pie_fk562_rpe1` has u 0.0299, STRING 0.0491, b 0.0588.

  **PIE's metrics**, mean ± sd over 4 folds, fill off: AUPRC 0.343 ± 0.025, Jaccard 0.203 ± 0.007, direction
  0.802 ± 0.013, Spearman effect size 0.652 ± 0.020, Spearman LFC 0.546 ± 0.032, Discrimination L1 0.746 ± 0.002.
  PIE as published: 0.304 ± 0.023 / 0.168 ± 0.011 / 0.775 ± 0.022 / 0.525 ± 0.030 / 0.552 ± 0.022 /
  0.709 ± 0.006.

  `ps:xatlas_hct116` was kept in all 12 fits. coess and depmap were kept in all 12.
- **Verdict:** kept as the first PIE comparison. The prediction was half right: the panel range was met, but the gap
  to matched was more than +0.03 on only 4 of 12 datasets. Superseded for the figure by K031 (PIE's five sources only).
- **For later work:** The pipeline transfers to PIE's split with no change. PIE's metrics are close to saturated
  between pools: the extended and matched pools differ by 0.003 AUPRC.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/K010-pie-extended-path/`, `lab/exp/K010-pie-extended-path-cal/` (archived: no), no findings
  entry.

### K011 · The like-for-like pool: no measured response to the gene (with K011-cal)
- **Question:** In PIE's test, the model has no measured transcriptional response to the held-out gene from anywhere.
  How does the pipeline score under the same condition?
- **Setup:** As K010, with 10 sources removed. The rule: drop a source if its features for gene g are an expression
  readout measured after perturbing g. Removed:
  - `ps:marson_rest`, `ps:marson_stim8`, `ps:marson_stim48`;
  - `ps:kolf_ipsc`;
  - `ps:xatlas_hct116`, `ps:xatlas_hek293t`;
  - `resp:l1000_shrna`, `resp:l1000_crispr`, `resp:l1000_orf`;
  - `sets:creeds`, whose perturbation sets include the perturbed gene itself.

  93 sources remain. Fitness screens, networks, gene sets, expression atlases, proteomics and embeddings stay. The
  arm asserts that no `ps:` or `resp:l1000` name remains.
- **Prediction:** Below K010 on most of the 12 datasets by more than 0.03 panel Pearson. Kept sets led by coess,
  depmap and complex or pathway sources.
- **Result:** Panel 300 Pearson Δ − k mean 0.519 against K010's 0.539, so 0.020 lower on average. The gap is more
  than 0.03 on only 4 of 12 datasets. coess, depmap and `net:string_textmining` were kept in 12 of 12 units,
  `net:coabundance` in 11.

  PIE's metrics (sd over 4 folds, fill off): 0.340 ± 0.024 / 0.191 ± 0.006 / 0.800 ± 0.008 / 0.621 ± 0.016 /
  0.544 ± 0.030 / 0.732 ± 0.005. That is above PIE's published row on 5 of 6 metrics and level on Spearman LFC.
- **Verdict:** kept. This is the fair comparison with PIE, shown as "PWPA Extended Priors" in the researcher's
  figure. The prediction was refuted in size.
- **For later work:** The benchmark lead over PIE does not come from other lines' Perturb-seq. Literature sources
  (`net:string_textmining`, `net:pubtator3`) stay in this pool and can describe a knockdown in words. Nobody checked
  this against PIE's inputs.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/K011-pie-matched-path/`, `lab/exp/K011-pie-matched-path-cal/` (archived: no), no findings
  entry.

### K020 · The scorer: PWPA predictions under PIE's own metric code
- **Question:** Can the dumps be scored with PIE's metric code so that the numbers sit in PIE's Table 4?
- **Setup:** PIE's code is imported unmodified from a clone at `21a526a`; `metrics.py` hash is logged and PIE's 39
  tests pass in the lab venv.
  - Truth: `de_true = (fdr < 0.05) & tested`, FDR 0.05 as in PIE's `train.yaml`.
  - Aggregation: a fold is the mean of its 3 units; the table is mean ± sample sd over the 4 folds.
  - **Label transfer:** for an arm, p_de = W @ DE_train and lfc = W @ LFC_train.
  - Training knockdowns' own-gene labels are masked.
  - **Target fill** (off for the headline): sets p_de = 1 at the knocked-down gene.
  - Reference rows: `context_mean`, ranking by training DE frequency.
  - All test rows are scored ("rows all"), including 780 of 4,876 (16.0%) that are lab lockbox knockdowns. The
    researcher approved this in chat on 2026-10-06, for this benchmark only.
- **Prediction:** A context-mean reference must reproduce PIE's published Context mean where the metric depends only
  on the shift: Discrimination L1 0.503, and Spearman effect size undefined. The DE metrics need not match.
- **Result:**

  | row | AUPRC | Jaccard | direction | Sp. effect | Sp. LFC | Discr. L1 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | paper: Context mean | 0.082 ± 0.009 | 0.063 ± 0.011 | 0.675 ± 0.025 | NaN | 0.372 ± 0.035 | 0.503 ± 0.000 |
  | `context_mean`, fill off | 0.282 ± 0.023 | 0.082 ± 0.012 | 0.705 ± 0.015 | NaN | 0.410 ± 0.047 | 0.503 ± 0.000 |
  | `context_mean`, fill on | 0.409 ± 0.007 | 0.082 ± 0.012 | 0.753 ± 0.006 | −0.062 ± 0.034 | 0.544 ± 0.022 | 0.503 ± 0.000 |
  | K010 extended, fill off | 0.343 ± 0.025 | 0.203 ± 0.007 | 0.802 ± 0.013 | 0.652 ± 0.020 | 0.546 ± 0.032 | 0.746 ± 0.002 |
  | K011 matched, fill off | 0.340 ± 0.024 | 0.191 ± 0.006 | 0.800 ± 0.008 | 0.621 ± 0.016 | 0.544 ± 0.030 | 0.732 ± 0.005 |
  | paper: PIE | 0.304 ± 0.023 | 0.168 ± 0.011 | 0.775 ± 0.022 | 0.525 ± 0.030 | 0.552 ± 0.022 | 0.709 ± 0.006 |

  Other published rows in the scorer's table (AUPRC): COMPASS-N 0.092, PRESAGE 0.091, TabICL 0.094.

  Other results:
  - With the fill, K010 reads 0.468 / 0.203 / 0.851 / 0.652 / 0.674 / 0.746.
  - On non-lockbox rows, every metric of the arms moves by 0.013 or less.
  - Using |P| as the DE score instead of label transfer gives AUPRC 0.319 for K010 and 0.315 for K011.
  - A perfect prediction scores AUPRC 0.985 in fold k562, because 17 rows have no DE gene.
  - Discrimination L1 matched the paper (0.5027) and Spearman effect size matched (NaN). AUPRC did not match and was
    not expected to: PIE adds a baseline's shift to 500 control cells and re-runs a DE test, and the lab did not
    reproduce that step.
- **Verdict:** infrastructure, validated. The README's header still says "no arm scored"; its later Result section has
  all 24 dumps.
- **For later work:**
  - Always show the training-DE-frequency row (AUPRC 0.282) beside the published Context mean (0.082).
  - Keep the fill off.
  - PIE publishes no per-fold values, so the fold sd (AUPRC 0.023–0.025) is the only spread available.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/K020-pie-score/` (archived: no), no findings entry.

### K030 · PIE's five genetic knowledge sources as PWPA similarity channels
- **Question:** Can PWPA be given exactly the gene-level information PIE's model gets on Replogle–Nadig?
- **Setup:** PIE's exact vectors were downloaded from HF `arcinstitute/PIE_sources` at commit `fb624a5` (43 GB,
  every sha256 matched). Each gene is resolved with PIE's own `SourceLookup`. Raw similarity is the lab's choice:

  | source | PIE's vector | similarity |
  |---|---|---|
  | `pie5:perturbation_text` | NCBI Gene entry text, OpenAI embedding (3,072) | cosine |
  | `pie5:ncbi_text` | GO, OMIM and gene groups as text; Qwen3.5 hidden states (2,048 per token) | cosine of the token mean |
  | `pie5:esm2` | ESM2 650M mean over residues (1,280) | cosine |
  | `pie5:string_space` | STRING v12 SPACE embedding (512) | cosine |
  | `pie5:depmap_gene_effect` | Chronos 25Q3 gene effect over 1,186 lines | Pearson |

  Nothing is fitted.
- **Prediction:** none recorded.
- **Result:** All five were built. Each covers 1,804–1,857 of PIE's 1,857 perturbations.
  - `ncbi_text` cosine sits near 1 for every pair (mean 0.957, sd 0.026).
  - ESM2 is also high (mean 0.861).
  - DepMap here is the raw gene effect, so CCT2's top correlations are only 0.16–0.21.
  - CCT2's top partners are other CCT/TCP1 subunits in four of the five sources.
- **Verdict:** infrastructure. It is used by K031, K034 and R033, and its priors are what the clean repo's main
  figure uses.
- **For later work:** Standardising over training pairs removes the offset of `ncbi_text`, not its compression.
  PIE's DepMap source is weaker than the lab's `coess`.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/K030-pie-five-sources/` (archived: no), no findings entry.

- **Question:** How does the lab's method do with its own sources and its own raw similarities for the same five

- **Result:** The README says "(pending)", and K031's README says K032 "was stopped after 9 of 12 units". On the pool,
  12 dumps exist, but only 4 units were scored (`POOL/lab/pie_bench/scores_K03x_k562/per_context__all.tsv`, fill off,
