# 2 · The gated model

Question: what is the simplest model that takes any number of external datasets through one input contract, gives
each a learned non-negative gate, uses one gene-to-gene graph for both output metrics, yields a readable gene
regulatory network (GRN), and still scores about as well as F034, the session-6 MLP scorer? This is session 8 of the
lab loop (2026-10-04 23:15 to 2026-10-05 09:10 EDT; charter "Session 8", spec
`docs/specs/2026-10-04-gated-one-graph-grn.md`, findings #155–#184), plus the experiments the researcher asked for on
2026-10-05 and 2026-10-06, outside the loop (H012–H014, M001; findings #185 only for H014). This is where PWPA's form
appeared: a weighted average of measured knockdowns, with weights from entmax-1.5 over a gated sum of standardised
prior similarities. Data: Marson CD4+ T-cell Perturb-seq, three states (Rest, Stim8hr, Stim48hr), 3,341 state ×
knockdown rows over 1,860 knocked-down genes, 11,526 screened genes (the universe), about 10,000 readout genes, with
Nadig Jurkat and Song Jurkat as external checks. Metric: the session-5 fixed panel (below) on the authors' DESeq2
z-scores and log2FC for H001–H012. From H013x on, the researcher's directive of 2026-10-05 moved fitting and scoring to
normalised expression. The two are different scales; this file never sets them side by side as one.

## Terms and the model form

Abbreviations, as the lab used them:
- **Δ_j**: the measured response of training knockdown j (a vector over readout genes). **t**: the held-out target
  knockdown being predicted.
- **k (k_c)**: the mean training knockdown response in state c. **b**: the baseline that predicts k. **r**: replicate
  agreement, one donor half against the other (dev panel 300: 0.397 on z; 0.370 on expression).
- **Pearson Δ − k**: per held-out knockdown, Pearson between prediction − k and truth − k on the panel, then the mean
  over knockdowns. **MSE**: raw mean squared error on the panel, in log2FC (z era) or in normalised expression.
- **Panel 300 / panel 100**: a fixed set of 300 (or 100) readout genes per state and fold, ranked from the training
  knockdowns only (session 5's fix of the old biased per-row top-N metric). Panel 300 is primary. The old metric is not
  used in this stage; `results.tsv`'s `pearson` columns are not quoted here.
- **dev**: 4-fold cross-validation over gene folds 0–3 (about 660 training knockdowns per state per fold).
  **lockbox**: gene fold 4, scored once at the end.
- **Retrieval**: a diagnostic only (rank of the knockdown's own truth by similarity; a shared prediction scores 0.5).
- **STRING (E000)**: the zero-parameter STRING top-20 neighbour average, 0.183 / 0.144 on dev z. In expression space the
  same comparator is called **Fawkes** (0.166 / 0.0221 dev).
- **F034**: F026's MLP pair scorer (15 inputs: 6 similarities, 6 "both genes present" flags, a 3-way state one-hot;
  15 → 32 → 32 → 1) with one attention graph for z and log2FC. Dev 0.3114 / 0.1035. **F009**: an additive learned
  kernel, ~20 parameters, 0.298 / 0.105. **E071a**: a phenocopy GRN prior, 0.234 / 0.122.
- Datasets (channels): **coess** DepMap co-essentiality, **depmap** raw DepMap gene effects, **k562** Replogle K562
  Perturb-seq profiles, **tcell** 37 primary T-cell CRISPR screens from 11 studies (E109d's matrix), **label** LLM
  programme labels, **string** STRING edges. Short form D K T L S.
- **perm0**: the whole-model control with every source's gene identities shuffled (`srcs.PERM = 0`).
  **Decoy**: a copy of one channel with gene identities permuted, added beside the real one during selection.
  **W (selection)**: a channel's gate minus its decoy's gate.
- **λ_max**: the smallest L1 penalty at which every gate is 0. **1-SE rule**: the largest λ whose 5-seed mean inner
  validation loss is within one SE of the best. **Decoy-calibrated rule (H003b's `rule()`)**: walk down the λ path and
  stop before the first λ at which any decoy is on in more than 1 of 5 seeds; keep real channels with gate > 0 in ≥ 4
  of 5 seeds there.
- **Fits**: 4 folds × 5 seeds = 20 fits. "Kept in x / 20" counts fits with gate > 0.
- GRN export (H008): **S** the prior graph Σ_d gate_d · sim_d over the universe; **W_c** the borrowing graph (entmax
  weights of every universe gene over state c's knocked-down genes); **G_c** the signed regulator → readout table.
  **no_info**: a regulator whose row is uniform (G = k). **Hubs**: memory genes that get many times the uniform weight.
  **No-profile genes**: knocked-down genes covered only by label and STRING. **TCR-proximal set**: CD247, LAT, CD3D,
  LCP2, LCK.

The model (spec §3; H001, H002, H012-drop-mean READMEs):
1. **Input contract.** Each dataset d gives one pair similarity sim_d(t, j): Pearson r of two genes' profiles for
   profile datasets, 1 / 0 for a shared label or a STRING edge. Each is centred and scaled over the training pairs where
   both genes are present (mean 0, SD 1; self pairs excluded; STRING over all training pairs). A missing pair gets 0,
   the average pair. There are no present flags. Call the result z_d(t, j).
2. **Score.** score(t, j) = Σ_d gate_d · z_d(t, j), with gate_d ≥ 0, clipped at 0 after each Adam step, starting at 0.
   One gate vector is shared by all three states (form A); there is no state input.
3. **Weights.** a_t = entmax-1.5 over the state's training knockdowns j, with t itself masked. The weights are ≥ 0,
   sum to 1 and are sparse (about 200 of 660 partners get weight). With every gate at 0 they are uniform, and the
   prediction is b.
4. **Size form (H002–H012).** P_t = k_c + s_c · Σ_j a_tj (Δ_j − k_c), with one size s_c per state. The three z sizes
   are learned; the log2FC prediction reuses the same a_tj, and its three sizes are set in closed form (as in F034).
   Learned numbers = n gates + 3 sizes (H002 and H004b: 6 + 3 = 9).
5. **Plain form (H012-drop-sizes, H012-drop-mean, H013-plain on; this is PWPA).** s_c = 1 (`SIZE_LR = 0`, the
   closed-form size fixed at 1) and no centring on k_c (`gated.CENTRE = False`): P_t = Σ_j a_tj Δ_j. Because the weights
   sum to 1, k_c + Σ_j a_tj (Δ_j − k_c) = Σ_j a_tj Δ_j, so dropping the mean changes nothing once the size is 1
   (H012-drop-mean equals H012-drop-sizes to four decimals). Fitted numbers = one gate per dataset. The same weights
   apply unchanged to z, log2FC and expression.
6. **Fit.** Loss = MSE on z (later expression) on the training-panel genes; 25% of training genes held out as inner
   validation for early stopping (best checkpoint); 5 seeds averaged; Adam, learning rate 3e-3, no weight decay.
7. **Selection (H003 on).** An L1 penalty λ · Σ_d gate_d is used only to select: fit a path from λ_max down to 0
   (λ_max / 2, / 4 … / 128, 0), pick a λ, keep the channels with gate > 0, then refit those with no penalty. The refit
   is the model. The clean repo later replaced this with RFE (recursive feature elimination).

## What this stage established

1. **A linear gated scorer with nine learned numbers is level with the MLP scorer.** Form A (H002, 6 gates + 3 sizes)
   scored 0.2900 / 0.1051 on dev panel 300, −0.0214 ± 0.0047 Pearson against F034. The gap split into the dropped
   present flags (H001, −0.0139 ± 0.0038, almost all Rest and coverage, not biology) and linearity (−0.008 ± 0.003).
   Changing only the T-cell channel to its plain form (H004b) gave 0.2969 / 0.1034: −0.015 ± 0.005 against F034 with
   equal MSE (−0.0002 ± 0.0004), "equal" by the session's margin at panel 300, 0.002 outside it at panel 100.
   (H001, H002, H004b; #159, #161, #172.)
2. **The pick replicated on the lockbox.** H004b on fold 4: 0.311 / 0.0937, +0.111 ± 0.013 over STRING (0.200), MSE
   −0.036 ± 0.005 against STRING and 18% below b (0.1147). F034 on fold 4: 0.315 / 0.0934. Paired H004b − F034:
   −0.004 ± 0.010 Pearson, +0.0003 ± 0.0007 MSE (panel 100 −0.004 ± 0.011 / +0.0008 ± 0.0009): a tie. Later, on
   normalised expression, the plain form H013x-expr-plain scored 0.2905 ± 0.0154 / 0.0150 ± 0.0006 on the lockbox,
   +0.0919 ± 0.0137 over Fawkes (dev: +0.1045 ± 0.0073). The lead over the STRING neighbour average transferred to the
   new metric at a similar size. (#184; H013x-expr-plain.)
3. **The score is gene-specific.** perm0 of the pick: 0.022 / 0.1172 (b 0.1173); H002-perm0 0.014; F026-perm0 had been
   0.087 through its present flags. Replacing only the T-cell channel by its decoy cost −0.011 ± 0.003, and the decoy's
   gate was exactly 0 in 19 of 20 fits with no penalty. (H004b-perm0, H004c; #174, #180.)
4. **L1 with the 1-SE rule is not a trustworthy selector; the decoy-calibrated rule is, but it is conservative.** Under
   the 1-SE rule the T-cell decoy was kept in 16 / 20 fits and in three of four fold sets, because the inner-validation
   loss keeps falling as the penalty relaxes. The calibrated rule kept coess, depmap, tcell, label and string in every
   fold, K562 in two, and refit 0.2861 (−0.0039 ± 0.0014 against H002). The refit score barely depends on the rule.
   (H003, H003-decoy1, H003b; #167, #169.)
5. **"Kept" means "not noise", not "useful", and gate size does not rank datasets.** IMPC was dropped (0 / 20). SIGNOR
   was kept in 18 / 20 fits and added 0.000 ± 0.001. The norm-only T-cell channel opened in 20 / 20 fits and added
   +0.001 ± 0.003. In drop-one refits of the pick, coess (gate 0.059) was worth +0.0003 ± 0.0027 once raw DepMap was in,
   while tcell:all (gate 0.031) was worth −0.0110 ± 0.0030 and depmap −0.0174 ± 0.0034. The six single-dataset losses
   sum to 0.052 against the model's 0.297, so the datasets are largely redundant. (H009, H004d, H012-explainer; #171,
   #176.)
6. **Splitting datasets buys nothing; few screens carry the signal; the plain product is the right T-cell form.** Split
   by study (n = 16) or by screen (n = 42), the mechanism held and the score did not move (H004 − H004a +0.0003 ±
   0.0014; H005 − H004a −0.002 ± 0.002). Calibrated sets kept 2–4 of the 37 screens (ORCS_1905 and ORCS_2424 in all
   folds). The plain mean of screen products beat the per-gene-normalised channel by +0.007 ± 0.003. Most of its value
   is the pairing (cosine alone +0.008 of the +0.011 over the decoy). Whitening the screens changed nothing (+0.001 ±
   0.002). (H004, H004a, H005, H004e, H004f; #170, #176, #179.)
7. **The PWPA form: sizes and the state mean can go.** Dropping both cost −0.0004 ± 0.0003 Pearson and nothing on MSE.
   With the label removed and the 37 screens as separate datasets, the decoy-calibrated plain model (H013-plain) scored
   0.290 / 0.1035 on z. Refit end to end on expression, it scored 0.2706 ± 0.0076 / 0.0154 ± 0.0003 (H013x-expr-plain,
   the model of record before the clean repo; −0.0018 ± 0.0022 against the z-fitted weights). (H012-drop-sizes,
   H012-drop-mean, H013-plain, H013x-expr-plain.)
8. **Additions that were measured and left out.**
   - A coverage input bought +0.0093 ± 0.0030 on Marson, but brought a third of F034's hubs back, added nothing on
     Nadig or Song (+0.0001 / +0.001), and vanished in the top-20 graph.
   - Per-state gates bought +0.003 to +0.006 (under the 0.01 bar), at the cost of three prior graphs.
   - Running the whole pipeline per state tied the pooled one (+0.0006 ± 0.0027, expression).
   (H002b, H006, H006b, H006c, H014; #162, #163, #165, #168, #175, #185.)
9. **A GRN reads straight out of the model and is hub-free, but its columns for never-knocked-down regulators are weak.**
   - Cut to 20 partners, the pick's graph alone scores 0.2818 / 0.1113, tied with F034's graph (0.281) and +0.098 over
     STRING. The largest memory gene gets 2.3–2.5× uniform weight (F034 11–19×).
   - The TCR-proximal genes find each other.
   - Against held-out FACS screens, columns for unmeasured regulators agree at mean Spearman +0.009 (pick) and +0.015
     (H002), clearing z ≥ 3 in 1 of 6 screens with a usable ceiling. 73% of the apparent agreement had been the screen
     coming back through the T-cell channel.
   (H008, H008c, H008g, H008f, H008i; #160, #164, #166, #173, #178.)
10. **The form is not the limit; the weights are.** An oracle that fits convex weights to one donor half predicts the
    other half at 0.408 against r 0.397. In that same unit the pick scores 0.249 and F034 0.262, so the gap to r is
    0.14, not 0.09. Outside Marson the pick keeps 93% (Nadig Jurkat) and 87% (Song Jurkat) of F034's gain over STRING.
    (H011, H011e, H011f, H010, H010c; #168, #173, #181–#183.)

## Experiments

### H001 · F034 with the input contract
- **Question:** what does the session-8 input contract (centred, scaled, missing = 0, no present flags) cost F034?
- **Setup:** Marson, dev, panel 300 / 100 on z / log2FC. F034's MLP with 9 inputs (6 standardised similarities + state
  one-hot; 9 → 32 → 32 → 1). D K T L S. Comparator F034 (paired). Control H001-perm0.
- **Prediction:** within 0.015 of F034 (≥ 0.296, guess 0.300–0.310), MSE ≤ 0.106; perm0 < 0.03.
- **Result:** 0.2975 / 0.1042 (Rest 0.245 / Stim8hr 0.303 / Stim48hr 0.344); panel 100 0.3142 / 0.0748. H001 − F034:
  −0.0139 ± 0.0038 (Rest −0.024, Stim8hr −0.004, Stim48hr −0.013), panel 100 −0.0164 ± 0.0040; MSE +0.0007 ± 0.0002.
  perm0 0.049 ± 0.007 (MSE 0.1171 = b), 0.038 ± 0.008 below F026-perm0 (0.087).
- **Verdict:** baseline / partly supported. It passes at panel 300 by 0.0015 and misses at panel 100. The loss tracks
  the coverage signal removed in each state.
- **For later work:** present flags carry coverage ("which genes are well studied"), mostly at Rest. An MLP can still
  find coverage from exact zeros; a linear form cannot.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/H001-input-contract/` (archived: no; H001-perm0 has no README), findings #161.

### H002 · Form A: the linear gated scorer
- **Question:** is the simple core (spec §3.3) close enough to F034?
- **Setup:** score = Σ_d gate_d · z_d over 6 channels, entmax-1.5, P_t = k_c + s_c · Σ_j a_tj (Δ_j − k_c). 6 gates + 3 z
  sizes learned (log2FC sizes closed form) = 9 numbers. No penalty. Dev panel 300 / 100. Comparators F034 and F009.
  Control H002-perm0. Learning rate 3e-3, picked on fold 0 (the choice hardly mattered).
- **Prediction:** 0.290–0.300, MSE ≤ 0.106; Rest ties F034, Stim8hr loses ≈ 0.02; perm0 < 0.03.
- **Result:** 0.2900 / 0.1051 (0.228 / 0.302 / 0.340); panel 100 0.3062 / 0.0756. vs F034 −0.0214 ± 0.0047 (Rest −0.041,
  Stim8hr −0.005, Stim48hr −0.018), MSE +0.0016 ± 0.0005; vs F009 −0.0081 ± 0.0058. perm0 0.014 (MSE 0.1172). Gates
  (mean ± SD, 20 fits): tcell 0.117 ± 0.025, k562 0.078 ± 0.023, depmap 0.065 ± 0.013, coess 0.051 ± 0.007, label 0.037 ±
  0.006, string 0.023 ± 0.003, all > 0 in 20 / 20. On perm0 the gates were as large but non-zero in only 3–14 of 20 fits.
  About 200–240 partners with weight per target.
- **Verdict:** kept as the base form; superseded as the pick by H004b. It is in the 0.015–0.03 trade-off band. The
  per-state prediction was wrong (the gap is Rest, not Stim8hr).
- **For later work:** stability across fits, not gate size, separates real channels from noise. Gates do not compare
  across channels (a STRING edge is 13.5 SD after scaling).
- **Source:** `lab/exp/H002-linear-gated/` (gated.py is the shared model code), findings #159.

### H002b · Form A plus a coverage input
- **Question:** does the spec's fallback, one shared "number of datasets covering this pair" input, recover H002's Rest
  loss?
- **Setup:** H002 + cov(t, j) = number of the five non-STRING channels covering both genes, entered as a mirrored
  pair cov+ / cov− so each gate stays ≥ 0. 8 gates + 3 sizes = 11 numbers. Comparator H002; also H001, F034.
- **Prediction:** 0.295–0.302, Rest ≥ 0.240, gate on cov− in ≥ 16 / 20 fits; perm0 0.04–0.09.
- **Result:** 0.2993 / 0.1048 (0.245 / 0.300 / 0.353); panel 100 0.3181 / 0.0753. vs H002 +0.0093 ± 0.0030 (Rest +0.017 ±
  0.008); vs H001 +0.0018 ± 0.0034; vs F034 −0.0121 ± 0.0035, MSE +0.0013 ± 0.0005. cov− > 0 in 20 / 20 (0.057 ± 0.022),
  cov+ in 3 / 20. perm0 0.037, with the sign flipped (cov+ 20 / 20). No-profile memory genes held 1.0–1.5% of
  attention (≈ 2× uniform).
- **Verdict:** dropped. It is inside the "equal" margin on every clause, but the gain is coverage, not biology. In the
  export it rebuilt hubs (3.5–3.9× uniform), it did not transfer (H010), and it was lost in the top-20 graph (H008e).
- **For later work:** a coverage count offsets "missing = 0" for poorly covered partners. Its perm0 cannot size the real
  gain because the two runs use the count with opposite signs.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/H002b-coverage-channel/`, findings #162, #164, #168.

### H003 · L1 select-then-refit with decoys
- **Question:** does L1 selection drop decoy channels and keep real ones, and does the refit score like H002?
- **Setup:** 12 channels (6 real + one decoy each, one shared permutation of the universe). Per fold a λ path (λ_max …
  / 128, 0) with 5 seeds; λ by the 1-SE rule; a fold's set = channels kept in ≥ 4 of 5 seeds; unpenalised refit.
  Controls: perm0, shufkept, shufdrop, shufdecoy, decoy1 (a second permutation draw).
- **Prediction:** every decoy in ≤ 2 / 20 fits and in no fold set; ≥ 4 of 6 real channels in ≥ 16 / 20; refit within
  ± 0.005 of H002.
- **Result:** refit 0.2891 / 0.1050, vs H002 −0.0009 ± 0.0011. All 6 real channels kept 20 / 20, W > 0 in every fit
  (tcell +0.099 … string +0.022). The chosen λ was λ_max / 64, / 64, 0, / 128 by fold. tcell_decoy was kept in 16 / 20
  fits and sat in three fold sets; string_decoy was in one. On the second draw (decoy1), tcell_decoy fell to 6 / 20.
  perm0: every gate 0, prediction = b (0.000 / 0.1173); its λ_max (0.19–0.23) was above the real run's chosen λ.
  Shuffling the kept tcell channel at prediction time cost −0.0090 ± 0.0045; shuffling the kept decoy cost −0.0003 ±
  0.0015. Path cost 51 min for 12 channels.
- **Verdict:** failed on selection, supported on score. By spec §6.2 this selection is not trusted.
- **For later work:** the inner-validation loss rewards less shrinkage, so the 1-SE rule picks almost no penalty.
  "Kept in ≥ 16 of 20 fits" is not a vote, because the fits share one decoy draw and most training genes. On the path,
  real channels enter long before any decoy (five at λ_max / 2, the first decoy at / 32).
- **Source:** `lab/exp/H003-l1-select-refit/` (arm.py holds `select`, `with_decoys`, the SETS knob), findings #167.

### H003b · Penalty set by the decoys
- **Question:** if λ is the smallest grid value at which no decoy is kept in more than 1 of 5 seeds, what survives, and
  what does the refit score?
- **Setup:** `rule()` applied to H003's stored path (no new path; training genes only), then H003's refit. Comparators
  H002, H003.
- **Prediction:** five or six real channels per fold, no decoy; within ± 0.005 of H002; with the second decoy draw the
  sets differ only in K562.
- **Result:** λ_max / 32 in every fold. Folds 0 and 2: coess, depmap, tcell, label, string; folds 1 and 3: all six. Refit
  0.2861 / 0.1052, vs H002 −0.0039 ± 0.0014; vs H003 −0.0030 ± 0.0018. On decoy1's path only the folds that lose K562
  change.
- **Verdict:** kept as the selection rule for the rest of the series (the "-cal" sibling arms), until RFE replaced it in
  the clean repo.
- **For later work:** trustworthy but conservative. K562 has the second-largest unpenalised gate but enters the path where
  noise enters, and is worth about 0.008 in a fold that drops it.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/H003b-decoy-calibrated/`, findings #169.

### H004 · T-cell channel split by study (n = 16), with the H004a control
- **Question:** does gate + L1 hold when the single T-cell channel becomes 11 study channels?
- **Setup:** channels.py rebuilt per-screen z-scores (pool `H004/V.npy`) because E109d's matrix had per-gene centring
  across screens. A study's similarity is the mean product of its screens' z-scores. 32 channels with decoys, H003's
  machinery, both rules. H004a (`tcell:all`, the plain mean of the 37 products, six channels) separates "split" from "no
  per-gene normalisation".
- **Prediction:** within ± 0.01 of H003; decoys dropped; 3–8 of 11 studies kept in ≥ 16 / 20 fits.
- **Result:** 1-SE 0.2972 / 0.1033; calibrated 0.2965 / 0.1034. H004 − H004a +0.0003 ± 0.0014 (1-SE), −0.0004 ± 0.0018
  (calibrated). H004a 0.2969 / 0.1034, vs H003 +0.0078 ± 0.0033 (Rest +0.021). Calibrated study sets: Schmidt and
  Chen in 4 / 4 folds, Carnevale 3, Shifrut 2, Liu 2, six studies never. The correlated pair Carnevale | Shifrut (r
  0.59–0.76) flipped between folds. 1-SE kept 1 decoy of 38 set members. Path 100 min.
- **Verdict:** supported; the split does nothing. The by-product (the plain channel is better) led to H004b.
- **For later work:** selection among near-duplicate datasets is not identified; the score does not care which one is
  kept. Heavy-tailed channels (kurtosis up to 854) did no visible harm, so the rank-to-normal variant was not run.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/H004-tcell-by-study/`, `lab/exp/H004a-tcell-all/`, findings #170.
