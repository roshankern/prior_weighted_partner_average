# 6 · Out-of-distribution data and LLM-built priors

This stage asked whether agent- or LLM-built, context-specific priors can make PWPA work on very
out-of-distribution (OOD) data. The first target was Shi mouse brain (`shi_brain_expr`): in vivo, adult mouse, three
neuron types, Cas9 knockouts read by single-nucleus RNA-seq. There PWPA's Pearson Δ − k with the expanded 100+ source
pool was 0.195, far below the replicate agreement r = 0.378. The work ran from 2026-10-14 to about 2026-10-22, outside
the nine numbered sessions. Most of it (K070–K095) ran on 2026-10-17 in one PI-agent session, the "OOD loop" (K070),
while the researcher was away. Datasets: Shi mouse brain first; Song Jurkat (stimulated T cells), Geiger BMDC (mouse
dendritic cells, LPS) and KOLF iPSC as confirmation sets; PIE's Replogle–Nadig lines and VCC H1 in the PIE
comparisons. The rule for this line: **no dataset selection**. Each arm is one unpenalised PWPA fit on all its
candidate sources, and the lab reports gate shares. This is an extension of PWPA, not the main path.

**Metric in force.** Every K05x and K07x–K09x number is the post-session-5 fixed-panel metric: Pearson Δ − k on a
panel of 300 readout genes chosen from training knockdowns (Song: panel 100), plus MSE. The biased session 1–4 metric
(each row's own top-N DE genes) is not used anywhere in this stage. K060–K063 use PIE's six metrics instead (AUPRC,
Jaccard, DE direction match, Spearman effect size, Spearman LFC, Discrimination L1, FDR 0.05). The two scales are never
compared directly below. Split: dev = folds 0–3 (Shi: 246 rows), lockbox = fold 4 (Shi: 57 rows, spent once in K085).

**Terms used in this file.**
- **Δ**: a knockdown's pseudobulk expression minus the control mean of its context (log1p CP10k).
- **k**: the context mean, i.e. the mean Δ of the context's training knockdowns.
- **Pearson Δ − k**: the Pearson correlation of predicted and measured Δ − k over the panel genes, averaged over rows.
- **b**: the baseline that predicts k for every row. It is quoted as its MSE.
- **r**: replicate agreement, mouse half A against mouse half B on the same rows. It is the noise ceiling.
- **Pooling and SE**: the mean of the three neuron-type means. SE = √(Σ state SEM²) / 3. Paired differences use the
  same rows and the same pooling.
- **PWPA PIE matched**: PWPA fit on PIE's five genetic sources (`perturbation_text`, `ncbi_text`, `esm2`,
  `string_space`, `depmap_gene_effect`, built for each dataset's genes).
- **PWPA 100+**: the session-9 expanded pool (91 sources after filters), selected with the L1 path and decoys
  (`plain_expanded`, J011).
- **PIE**: Arc Institute's published perturbation model (`ArcInstitute/pie`).
- **Fawkes**: the STRING v12 top-20 neighbour average (from Fawkes et al. 2026, arXiv 2606.08816).
- **TxPert**: the TxPert + STRING baseline.
- **OOD arm**: PIE matched plus `ood:shi_luna_text` (K051).
- **Gate share**: a source's gate divided by the sum of the gates (seed mean, then the mean over folds).
- **Option A**: a PIE-metric read-out of PWPA's weights. It is described in K060.
- **Biology screen (K055/K071)**, comparing each source with the measured response map of the dev genes:
  - **lift**: top-10 neighbour overlap divided by chance;
  - **ρ**: Spearman over gene pairs;
  - **alike**: of 18 literature "should phenocopy" pairs, how many are linked;
  - **apart**: of 12 literature pairs expected to differ, how many are wrongly linked (lower is better);
  - **module coherence**: over 25 literature modules;
  - **trap pairs**: Nr1d1–Rora (antagonists), Nfe2l1–Nrf1 (alias collision), Naa15–Naa20 and Atxn1–Atxn2 should be
    apart; Atxn1–Cic and Tsc1–Tsc2 should be together.
- **Bar**: the charter's success bar, a gain of ≥ +0.03 Pearson Δ − k over PIE matched (0.2325 ± 0.0175).
- **Winner's curse**: picking the best of many arms on the same 246 dev rows inflates its score.

**LLMs and cost.**
- Models, all from OpenAI:
  - `gpt-6-luna` ("Luna"; $0.10 in / $0.01 cached / $0.50 out per 1M tokens; K051 added $0.01 per web search);
  - `gpt-6-sol` ("Sol"; $2 / $10 per 1M), only in the K082 pilot;
  - `text-embedding-3-large` (3,072-d; $0.13 per 1M tokens).
- K051 alone cost $6.99.
- The 2026-10-17 OOD loop spent $23.60 of a $30 budget. The budget was raised from $10 that evening. The
  per-experiment spends below agree with that total.
- Calls went through `lab/llm.py` with a cache on disk and spend rows per experiment tag in `lab/spend/`.
- After K051, every call used strict JSON schemas and no web search.

**Sources.**

- The OOD loop's own log: `lab/exp/K070-ood-loop/{FINDINGS.md, STATE.md, results.tsv}`.

## What this stage established

1. **PIE's five sources beat the 100+ pool on Shi dev, but the lead did not show on the lockbox.**
   - On dev, PWPA PIE matched scored 0.2325 ± 0.0175, against 0.1950 ± 0.0190 for PWPA 100+. The paired difference
     was +0.0375 ± 0.0093 (K052). Both also beat Fawkes (0.1615) and TxPert (0.0705).
   - On the lockbox (57 rows) PIE matched scored 0.167 ± 0.041 and 100+ scored 0.172 ± 0.040 (K085), so the dev lead
     did not reproduce.
   - The lab still called it "the real Shi gain" (K070 finding 8, open item b).
2. **Knowledge-only LLM priors plateau near +0.01 on Shi, far under the +0.03 bar.** Each line below is PIE matched
   plus the source, minus PIE matched:
   - Luna paragraph embedding +0.0034 ± 0.0037 (K051/K052, $6.99);
   - parsed "acts like" partners +0.0016 ± 0.0027 (K056);
   - signed program scores +0.0029 ± 0.0053 (K057);
   - ranked phenocopy lists +0.0005 ± 0.0061 (K072);
   - predicted signatures −0.0036 ± 0.0059 (K074); the same signatures centred +0.0081 ± 0.0056 (K077); signatures
     asked to be knockout-specific +0.0042 ± 0.0039 (K079);
   - effect-sentence embedding +0.0074 ± 0.0045 (K075);
   - stress sets +0.0050 ± 0.0028 (K081);
   - PIE's own text builder used to fill the missing genes −0.0031 ± 0.0031 (K084).
   - The best stack (claims + centred signatures + stress sets) reached +0.0122 ± 0.0062. It was picked on dev rows.
   - K070 findings 2, 5.
3. **The headroom is real; the bottleneck is how well a prior agrees with measured responses (K076, oracle).**
   - A prior built from mouse half B's measured responses adds +0.137 ± 0.014, scored on the clean half-A scale (PIE
     matched 0.192 → 0.329, against a ceiling of 0.378).
   - Its sparse top-10 graph adds +0.091 ± 0.013.
   - The oracle's lift is 6–11. Every LLM prior sits at a lift of about 2–2.5 (K070 finding 1).
4. **Showing the LLM the training responses (retrieval) failed twice.**
   - K078: the honest (directed) channel scored −0.0166 ± 0.0076, and real minus the permuted-response control was
     −0.0157 ± 0.0074. The LLM mapped most genes onto five stress hubs.
   - K080 added abstention. The hubs went away, but the gain did too: +0.0008 ± 0.0033, and real − control was
     −0.0040 ± 0.0037.
   - A symmetrised partner graph built from prompts that show other genes' responses leaks during gate fitting. Use
     directed channels only (K070 finding 4).
5. **A stronger model or more reasoning does not help (K082, 20-gene pilot).**
   - Partner precision against the measured top-10: Luna 0.250, Luna high effort 0.279, Sol 0.232.
   - Sol costs about 20× more per gene. All three gave the same trap answers (K070 finding 3).
6. **The one LLM prior that clearly transfers is the simplest: dense pair scores (K086, K092).**
   - Method: one Luna call per gene that scores 0–4 how similar every other gene's knockout response would be, with
     two samples.
   - Shi: +0.0129 ± 0.0033.
   - Three pre-registered new datasets: Song +0.0179 ± 0.0059, Geiger +0.0267 ± 0.0105, KOLF +0.0045 ± 0.0064.
     Pooled +0.0139 ± 0.0040; with Shi +0.0133 ± 0.0025. MSE is lower on all four.
   - It is still under the +0.03 bar, and alone it does not replace PIE off Shi.
   - LLM reranking of PIE's own top neighbours (K087/K091, pooled +0.0075 ± 0.0025) and centred signatures (K077/K090,
     pooled +0.0072 ± 0.0034) give about half as much.
   - More samples or a signed scale do not improve the pair score (K095). K070 findings 9, 10, 13.
7. **Fawkes-style in-context RL mostly failed.**
   - K089: RL on free LLM lists raised training reward but lost on test (−0.0057 ± 0.0071).
   - K094: the Fawkes action, in which the LLM edits STRING's top-10 neighbours, works. The held-out neighbour average
     improved by +0.0211 ± 0.0069, but most of that came before any RL (round 0 +0.0126 ± 0.0082). The RL step added
     +0.0084 ± 0.0063, which is not significant.
   - As a PWPA channel the edited set gave +0.0108 ± 0.0045 (K070 finding 12).
8. **Better biology does not mean a better score, and gate share is not contribution.**
   - K072's lists had the best lift (2.51) and fixed 5 of 7 traps, but gained +0.0005.
   - Luna text alone scores 0.209, near STRING alone (0.221), yet adds only +0.0034 to the full set: it is redundant
     (K054 Shapley).
   - DepMap holds 25.5% of the gate but 15% of the score (K054).
   - The largest convergent cluster (ER and integrated stress) is only about 30–60 of the 246 rows, so even perfect
     linking there gives at most about +0.01 to +0.02 pooled (K070 finding 7).
9. **Under PIE's metrics, PIE wins on Shi once it is trained on Shi with its own pipeline.**
   - Released PIE checkpoints, given Shi's training rows as evidence, score below the context mean (AUPRC 0.102 vs
     0.147, fold 0, K060).
   - With PIE's pipeline and labels on 4 folds, PIE beats PWPA matched (option A) on 5 of 6 metrics (AUPRC 0.187 ±
     0.023 vs 0.125 ± 0.007, K061).
   - On the lab's pseudobulk labels, PWPA led on fold 0 (K060).
   - Across 8 datasets, PWPA matched has the higher AUPRC on 5: K562, RPE1, HepG2, Jurkat and VCC H1. PIE is higher on
     the three OOD sets: KOLF, Shi and Geiger (K062).
10. **The lockbox did not confirm the best stack (K085, one shot, 57 rows).**
    - The best stack scored 0.183 ± 0.040 and PIE matched 0.167 ± 0.041.
    - The paired difference was +0.015 ± 0.023, the same size as on dev (+0.012), but its SE is too wide to confirm it.
    - Shi's lockbox is now spent, so later gains were confirmed on Song, Geiger and KOLF dev folds instead.

## Experiments

### K050 · PIE's five sources for Shi's genes
- **Question:** build PIE's five genetic sources for the 1,908 Shi genes so that PWPA can be fit "PIE matched" on
  Shi. It reuses K030's builder; only the gene list changes.
- **Setup:** PIE's source files at `fb624a5` and PIE's own lookup (name, then alias). Unit rows; DepMap is centred
  first. No responses are used.
- **Prediction:** none recorded.
- **Result:** of the 191 kept-row genes, the sources cover:
  - `perturbation_text` 113 (956 of 1,908 genes overall);
  - `ncbi_text` 191;
  - `esm2` 191;
  - `string_space` 189;
  - `depmap_gene_effect` 191.
  
  `perturbation_text` only has keys for genes perturbed in PIE's own datasets, and it has no alias file. `ncbi_text`
  and `esm2` similarities are compressed near 1 (pair mean 0.955 and 0.876).
- **Verdict:** infrastructure.
- **For later work:** `perturbation_text` misses about 40% of an OOD gene set. K084 showed that filling the gap does
  not help.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/K050-shi-pie5-sources/` (archived: no).

### K051 · Luna writes neuron-type-specific gene text (`ood:shi_luna_text`)
- **Question:** can an LLM with web search write a context-specific prior for an OOD dataset?
- **Setup:**
  - Prompt structure: one `gpt-6-luna` call per gene (191 genes) through the Responses API, with web search.
  - Domains of the Shi atlas and its press coverage were blocked.
  - Each call writes one paragraph of about 120 words for each of the three neuron types. A paragraph covers the
    gene's role there, its pathway, the expected knockout transcriptome change, and which genes the knockout would act
    like.
  - A strict leakage rule forbids the Shi atlas and any pooled brain screen of the gene.
  - The three paragraphs are concatenated and embedded with `text-embedding-3-large`. The source is the embeddings
    centred over the 191 genes, with unit rows.
- **Prediction:** none recorded.
- **Result:**
  - 192 calls: 6,704,322 input tokens (918,125 of them cached), 537,446 output tokens (343,266 of them reasoning), and
    610 web searches. **Total cost $6.99.** Wall time 23 min.
  - The leakage screen found one gene, Ddx39b, that had consulted (not cited) a copy of the atlas. Ddx39b was
    regenerated, and the final screen was clean.
  - Many paragraphs hedge ("predictions, not measured").
- **Verdict:** kept as a source. Its value is scored in K052, and it was superseded by K086.
- **For later work:** the web-searched text ($6.99) cost far more than the later no-search prompts (K086: $0.509) and
  added less. Screen consulted URLs, not only cited ones: the first regex missed URL slugs written with underscores.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/K051-shi-luna-ood/` (archived: no).

### K052 · PWPA on Shi: PIE matched and OOD (+ Luna text), no selection
- **Question:** do PIE's sources, and then Luna's text, improve PWPA on Shi compared with the 100+ pool?
- **Setup:**
  - Data: Shi dev folds 0–3, 246 rows, fixed panel 300.
  - Model: one unpenalised fit on all of an arm's sources (no L1 path, decoys or RFE). The fit is J011-cal's refit
    (5 seeds, best inner-validation checkpoint, one gate per source). It fits 5 gates for PIE matched and 6 for OOD.
  - Check: the same code reproduces PWPA 100+ to 6e-7 per row.
- **Prediction:** none recorded.
- **Result (Pearson Δ − k / MSE):**

  | arm | Pearson Δ − k | MSE |
  |---|---|---|
  | TxPert | 0.0705 ± 0.0180 | 0.02495 |
  | Fawkes | 0.1615 ± 0.0174 | 0.02528 |
  | PWPA 100+ | 0.1950 ± 0.0190 | 0.01890 |
  | PIE matched | 0.2325 ± 0.0175 | 0.01898 |
  | OOD (PIE matched + Luna text) | 0.2359 ± 0.0180 | 0.01886 |
  | r / b MSE | 0.378 | 0.02086 |

  Paired differences:
  - PIE matched − 100+: **+0.0375 ± 0.0093**;
  - OOD − PIE matched: **+0.0034 ± 0.0037**;
  - MSE: no change.

  Gate shares in the OOD arm: STRING 27.7%, DepMap 24.3%, Luna 20.9%, perturbation_text 11.7%.
- **Verdict:** PIE matched kept as the Shi comparator. The Luna text failed: it takes a fifth of the gate and adds
  nothing readable.
- **For later work:** every later OOD arm is "PIE matched + new source" against 0.2325 ± 0.0175.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/K052-shi-pwpa-ood/` (archived: no).

### K054 · Exact Shapley value of each source on Shi
- **Question:** how much of K052's score does each source carry?
- **Setup:**
  - K052's fit on all 63 non-empty subsets of the 6 sources × 4 folds (252 fits).
  - Exact Shapley value per row, pooled as in K052. The empty coalition scores 0 (it predicts k).
- **Prediction:** none recorded.
- **Result (Shapley value, Pearson Δ − k):**

  | source | PIE matched | OOD (6 sources) |
  |---|---|---|
  | STRING | 0.0987 ± 0.0104 | 0.0754 ± 0.0076 |
  | perturbation_text | 0.0441 ± 0.0059 | 0.0334 |
  | ncbi_text | 0.0370 | 0.0289 |
  | DepMap | 0.0350 | 0.0253 |
  | ESM2 | 0.0178 | 0.0145 |
  | Luna | — | 0.0583 ± 0.0074 |

  Single sources alone: STRING 0.221, Luna 0.209, NCBI 0.147, perturbation_text 0.139, DepMap 0.127, ESM2 0.083.

- **Source:** `lab/exp/K054-shi-shapley/` (archived: no).

### K055 · Shi prior map: how each prior clusters the 191 genes against the measured response
- **Question:** which priors agree with the measured response map on Shi dev genes?
- **Setup:**
  - Measured map: the per-state Δ − k cosine on dev folds 0–3.
  - Comparisons: kNN with k = 10, Louvain clusters, top-10 overlap with the measured map, Spearman over pairs, and 30
    literature pairs.
  - Noise ceiling: mouse half A against half B.
- **Prediction:** none recorded.
- **Result:**
  - Lift: perturbation_text 1.70, ncbi_text 2.05, ESM2 1.35, STRING 2.15 (ρ 0.173, the best), DepMap 1.96, Luna
    2.09 (ρ 0.112).
  - The half A vs half B ceiling has a lift of 5.28 (ρ 0.423).
  - STRING and Luna link every alike pair but also link 1 and 2 of the 12 apart pairs.
- **Verdict:** infrastructure. It is the biology screen that K071 reuses. An HTML page; v2 is K083.
- **For later work:** every prior sits at about 2× chance, against about 5× for replicate halves.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/K055-shi-prior-map/` (archived: no).

### K056 · Partner graph parsed from Luna's "would act like" sentences
- **Question:** does a graph of the partners Luna named (K051's cached text, no new spend) beat embedding the whole
  paragraph? Bar: +0.03.
- **Setup:**
  - A rule-based parser over K051's paragraphs, with cue words and contrast handling. 108 pairs, 122 genes.
  - The graph is used as a J001 source with k = 20. K052's fit is used unchanged.
- **Prediction:** the bar of +0.03.
- **Result:**
  - PIE matched + partners: 0.2341 ± 0.0175, **+0.0016 ± 0.0027** vs PIE matched.
  - On the 89 rows that have a direct partner: +0.0072 ± 0.0076.
  - OOD + partners − OOD: +0.0011 ± 0.0015.
  - MSE: +0.00017 ± 0.00008 (worse).
  - Gate share 10–14%.
- **Verdict:** failed.
- **For later work:** LLM-named partners are mostly same-complex or same-pathway genes (Tsc1–Tsc2, Mef2a–Mef2c),
  which STRING already links. Coverage was not the limit.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/K056-shi-luna-partners/` (archived: no).

### K057 · Luna's text re-read as signed program scores
- **Question:** does re-reading the cached text as signed claims about programs, rather than as an embedding (which
  measures topic), give a better prior?
- **Setup:**
  - 35 fixed neuronal programs.
  - Luna, with no tools, reads each gene's three paragraphs and scores every program from −2 to +2 for each neuron
    type. Two passes, averaged.
  - The result is a source of 105 signed features. 382 calls, $0.416.
- **Prediction:** none recorded (bar +0.03).
- **Result:**
  - Programs + PIE matched: 0.2354 ± 0.0180, +0.0029 ± 0.0053 vs PIE matched.
  - Programs + text: +0.0049 ± 0.0057.
  - Programs + partners: +0.0048 ± 0.0061.
  - The two passes agree on 98.4% of cells. Only about 8% of cells are non-zero.
  - Biology: lift 1.99, alike 9/17, apart 8/10 (best apart).
- **Verdict:** failed.
- **For later work:** signed claims separate antagonists and alias collisions, but they are too sparse and too generic
  ("synaptic down") to link true partners.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/K057-shi-luna-programs/` (archived: no).

### K060 · Released PIE checkpoints on Shi; PIE trained on Shi; PWPA read-outs for PIE's metrics
- **Question:** can the released PIE checkpoints be scored fairly on Shi, with Shi's training knockdowns as evidence,
  next to the context mean and PWPA matched? Is PIE trained on Shi any better?
- **Setup:**
  - Metric: PIE's six metrics through PIE's own code. Shi dev fold 0 (59 test rows); labels are the lab's pseudobulk
    DE (pydeseq2, mouse block).
  - PIE: Arc's four `PIE_replogle_wdataset` checkpoints. A check on Replogle reproduces each checkpoint's shipped AUPRC within 0.0003.
  - PWPA matched: K052's fit, with its weights W dumped.
  - Option A reads DE calls and LFC off W the way PIE's metrics read them. Each row calls its top n genes by
    W @ DE_train, where n is the partners' DE count. LFC is taken given DE. Nothing is refit.
- **Prediction:** none recorded.
- **Result (fold 0; order: AUPRC / Jaccard / direction / Spearman effect size / Spearman LFC / Discrimination L1):**

  | model | metrics |
  |---|---|
  | context mean | 0.147 / 0.000 / 0.665 / NaN / 0.349 / 0.537 |
  | PIE released, mean of 4 checkpoints | 0.102 / 0.067 / 0.540 / −0.032 / 0.111 / 0.546 |
  | PWPA matched | 0.183 / 0.024 / 0.706 / 0.404 / 0.388 / 0.579 |

  On PIE's exact 150/37 split:
  - PIE trained on Shi (best step 400): 0.159 / 0.081 / 0.653 / 0.133 / 0.418 / 0.560;
  - PWPA matched: 0.175 / 0.028 / 0.698 / 0.201 / 0.349 / 0.579;
  - PWPA option A: 0.175 / 0.090 / 0.693 / 0.252 / 0.395 / 0.579, leading PIE on 5 of 6 metrics.

  Choosing PWPA's stopping step by validation AUPRC instead of validation loss changes nothing that matters (test
  AUPRC 0.174 vs 0.175).
- **Verdict:** superseded by K061, which reverses the result on PIE's own labels.
- **For later work:**
  - Released PIE weights do not transfer to mouse brain: their direction match of 0.54 is near chance.
  - PIE's Jaccard lead came from over-calling DE.
  - One fold and no SE.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/K060-shi-pie-pretrained/` (archived: no).

### K063 · PIE's paper protocol on Replogle–Nadig and four datasets ("Table 4")

### K094 · Fawkes-style RL: the LLM edits STRING's top-10 neighbours
- **Question:** with Fawkes's own action (start from a knowledge-graph neighbour set; the LLM adds and removes members)
  and leak-free demonstrations, does in-context RL beat the unedited set?
- **Setup:**
  - Base: PIE's `string_space` top-10 among fold-f training genes.
  - Extras offered for adding: string_space ranks 11–20 plus the top K086 pairscore candidates.
  - Up to 5 removals and 5 additions per row.
  - Reward: P(edited) − P(base) on training rows. Demonstrations come from one half of the training genes; a policy is
    accepted only at ≥ 1 paired SE on the other half. Demonstrations touching the query are filtered out.
  - Cost $3.79.
- **Prediction:** round 0 about the unedited score; test final − unedited about +0.005; PWPA about +0.003.
- **Result:**
  - Held-out neighbour average: unedited 0.1819 ± 0.0147, round 0 0.1946, final 0.2030 ± 0.0155.
  - **Final − unedited +0.0211 ± 0.0069** (all 4 folds positive). Round 0 − unedited +0.0126 ± 0.0082. **The RL step
    added +0.0084 ± 0.0063** (1.3 SE).
  - PWPA: PIE matched + edited channel **+0.0108 ± 0.0045**; RL added +0.0022 ± 0.0030.
- **Verdict:** partly supported. The edit action works, and most of the gain needs no RL. The RL line ended here.
- **For later work:**
  - Round-0 edits are the cheap win; part of them comes from the pairscore candidates.
  - They were not confirmed off Shi.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/K094-shi-icl-rl-edit/` (archived: no).

### K095 · Two pair-score variants: 4 samples, or a signed −2..+4 scale
- **Question:** does the pair score improve with more samples (V1), or with a scale that can say "opposite" (V2)?
