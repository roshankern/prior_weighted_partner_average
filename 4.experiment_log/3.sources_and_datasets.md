# 3 · More sources, more datasets

This stage asked two questions. Does the frozen H013-plain pipeline work unchanged on ten Perturb-seq datasets?
Does a larger candidate pool help it, grown from 41 prior sources to 119? The work is session 9 of the lab loop
(night of 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-06), the J series (J001–J113; J020, the TxPert comparator, is covered in another
file). It also covers the session-7 groundwork it reused: G002 (the external-dataset scorer) and G003–G005
(Marson-style preprocessing, Nadig Jurkat, Song Jurkat). The ten datasets are:
- Marson primary CD4 T cells;
- Replogle K562 and RPE1;
- Nadig HepG2 and Jurkat;
- Song Jurkat (stimulated);
- KOLF2.1J iPSC;
- VCC H1 hESC;
- Shi mouse brain in vivo;
- Geiger-Schuller mouse bone-marrow dendritic cells.

Four more candidates failed the usability rule. The researcher's rule for the session was "plug data in, change
nothing else". The model, the loss, the selection and the metric were frozen. New code only loaded sources and
prepared datasets.

**Metric in force (session-5 fixed panel, in normalised expression).** Each knockdown's response Δ is a pseudobulk
mean of log1p(counts scaled to 10,000 over the readout genes) minus the control mean. The own gene is zeroed.
- **k** is the context mean: the mean training-knockdown response of the state.
- **Pearson Δ − k** is the Pearson correlation of (prediction − k) with (truth − k), on a fixed panel of 300 genes
  chosen from the training knockdowns (**panel 300**; Song is read at **panel 100** because it measures only 374
  genes). Higher is better.
- **MSE** is on the same panel. Lower is better.
- **b** is the baseline that predicts k for every knockdown. It scores 0 on Pearson Δ − k by construction.
- **r** is the replicate agreement: one half of the cells (lane blocks, donors, guides or mice) against the other
  half. It was called "the roof" during the session. It is a noisy half-data figure, not a ceiling: models can pass
  it.
- **c** is the noise ceiling, sqrt(2r / (1 + r)) per knockdown.
- The effect-size bar for a real gain is **+0.03** Pearson Δ − k, paired by knockdown.

All numbers are on the **dev split** (gene folds 0–3, seed 0). **Fold 4 (the lockbox)** of every dataset stayed
sealed all session. Nothing here uses the old biased metric of sessions 1–4 or the PIE metrics of the K series.
Session-7 numbers (G004/G005) are in DE z-score space, not normalised expression, so they are not on the same
scale as the J numbers.

**Terms used throughout.**
- **H013-plain** is the model of record that entered the session. It predicts P_t = Σ_j a_tj Δ_j over the state's
  training knockdowns, with a = entmax-1.5(Σ_d gate_d · z_d(t,j)). Each z_d is source d's similarity, standardised
  over the training pairs. There is one non-negative gate per kept source and no size or mean term.
- **Selection** runs an L1 path over λ_max/2 … /128 and 0 with 5 seeds. Every candidate gets a **decoy**, a copy of
  the source with its gene identities shuffled. **H003b's decoy-calibrated rule** picks the set, which is then
  **refit** without the penalty.
- **The arm families.** The "-path" arms select and the "-cal" arms give the quoted number. **R002** is the same
  pipeline's earlier run on Replogle K562.
- **Fawkes** (arm E000) is the STRING top-20 neighbour average.
- **TxPert + STRING** is a trained graph network (J020).
- **current pool** means H013-plain's 41 sources: coess (co-essentiality), depmap, string, `k562` (Replogle K562
  Perturb-seq profiles) and 37 CRISPR screens.
- **expanded pool** means the 119 frozen names: 14 old (coess, depmap, string, k562 and 10 screens) plus 105 new.
  The 27 never-selected T-cell screens were cut.
- **Rule B** (the leakage rule) is defined in conclusion 6.
- **The coverage rule:** a new source enters a dataset's pool only if it covers at least 10% and at least 67 of
  the dev knockdowns.
- **The Marson date rule:** a knowledge source in Marson's pool must be released on or before 2025-12-23.
- **Marson's rule** keeps a perturbation with more than 75 changed genes (padj < 0.1) and more than 50 cells.
- **The usability rule:** a dataset is used only with at least 100 dev knockdowns and dev r ≥ 0.15.

## What this stage established

1. **The unchanged pipeline transfers to all ten datasets.** On its 41 old sources it beats Fawkes on nine
   datasets by +0.06 to +0.17 Pearson Δ − k (dev, panel 300, paired):
   - Marson +0.105 ± 0.007; K562 +0.079 ± 0.006; RPE1 +0.168 ± 0.012;
   - HepG2 +0.139 ± 0.010; Nadig Jurkat +0.148 ± 0.007; Song (panel 100) +0.062 ± 0.009;
   - KOLF +0.097 ± 0.015; VCC H1 +0.107 ± 0.016; Geiger +0.076 ± 0.023.

   On Shi mouse brain it is +0.018 ± 0.012, inside the SE band. It beats TxPert + STRING on all ten (+0.09 to
   +0.30), and its MSE is below b on all ten. (J010-current-path-cal; findings #188, #191, #193, #196, #197, #199.)
2. **The loader changed nothing.** Fed through the new source loader, the pipeline reproduces R002 on Replogle K562
   to every digit: Pearson Δ − k 0.59996 and MSE 0.01973 against R002-cal's 0.59996 / 0.01973, with the same kept
   sets in all four folds. So every new number differs from the old pipeline only by the data plugged in. (J001,
   J010; #186.)
3. **The expanded pool helps a lot only inside the Replogle/Nadig cancer-line family.** Expanded − current, Pearson
   Δ − k, paired, dev:
   - over the +0.03 bar: RPE1 **+0.104 ± 0.010** (finished after the session), HepG2 **+0.067 ± 0.008**, K562
     **+0.066 ± 0.006**, Nadig Jurkat **+0.040 ± 0.004**;
   - real but under the bar: VCC H1 +0.025 ± 0.012, Song +0.019 ± 0.006, Marson +0.015 ± 0.005;
   - not readable: Shi +0.016 ± 0.012, Geiger −0.003 ± 0.018, KOLF −0.023 ± 0.017.

   MSE moves the same way: RPE1 −0.0111 ± 0.0011, HepG2 −0.0057 ± 0.0006, K562 −0.0031 ± 0.0003. (J011-cal, J012;
   #190, #191, #193, #197–#199; RPE1 from the J090 final log.)
4. **The gain comes from sibling cell lines' Perturb-seq, not from knowledge sources.** Where the expanded pool
   helps, 54–71% of the refit's gate weight sits on Perturb-seq channels of sibling screens: Replogle, Nadig and
   X-Atlas (K562 0.64, HepG2 0.54, Nadig Jurkat 0.71). On RPE1 the sets keep `ps:nadig_hepg2`, `ps:nadig_jurkat`
   and `ps:xatlas_hct116` in all four folds.

   Where the pool does not help, the share is under 12% (KOLF 0.07, Shi 0.12, Geiger 0.00), although the same
   sources are in most of those pools. On Song (0.36) and VCC H1 (0.35) the share is mostly or entirely the old
   `k562` channel. The ~90 knowledge, network, expression and embedding sources add at most about +0.02 where no
   sibling screen is kept. This is a soft leak by design: the sibling screens share guide libraries and labs.
   **The gain without any `ps:` source was never measured.** (J090 kept.tsv; #194.)
5. **More candidates did not make the selection worse.** No decoy was kept in any fold of the 20 dataset × pool
   runs. Kept sets grew from 2–9 sources per fold (current; Marson 7–9) to 1–14 (expanded). 60 different sources were kept at
   least once. STRING text mining (`net:string_textmining`) and PubTator co-mention (`net:pubtator3`) are the new
   knowledge sources kept most often. (#194, J090 final log.)
6. **Rule B worked as data, not code.** When dataset X is scored, a source leaves X's pool if it is derived from X,
   or if it is a Perturb-seq profile source (`kind == perturbseq`) of X's cell system. Other data of the same cell
   type stay. For example, Marson keeps its T-cell screens, and K562 loses only `k562` and Norman. The rule is read
   from `lab/sources.tsv` and `lab/datasets.tsv` by one function, `sources.pool()`. After rule B, the date rule and
   the coverage rule, the expanded pool is 81–108 sources per dataset. (J001; spec section 4; #187.)
7. **Four of fourteen candidate datasets failed the usability rule. No filter was relaxed.** Marson's rule passes
   too few perturbations:
   - Frangieh melanoma: 30 knockout × state rows over 20 genes;
   - Southard Hs27 CRISPRa: 96 targets, 77 dev;
   - Zhou CD8 TIL: 13 of 180 knockouts, 11 dev;
   - Joung TF Atlas ORF: 11 of 1,061 genes, 9 dev; 100 dev are needed.

   Song's Rest state also failed (r 0.027) and was dropped. The two reserves used were Geiger (for Zhou) and VCC H1
   (for the Frangieh → Southard → Joung slot). (J106, J108, J110, J112, J104; #187, #195.)
8. **Small datasets needed one new pool rule.** On 136–704 knockdowns, some sparse sources, or their decoys, have
   zero similarity spread over a fold's training pairs. The frozen fit asserts sd > 0, and three overnight fits died
   on it. The rule drops such a source for the whole dataset: 7 rows on KOLF and Song, none elsewhere. The kept
   channels are numerically unchanged. (J013; #192.)
9. **The 105 new sources range widely on a CORUM co-complex diagnostic, and it did not predict use.** The frozen pool
   holds 105 new sources (Perturb-seq 18 including the datasets' own, fitness/screens 15, gene sets 24, networks 24,
   expression/proteome/response/embedding 19, regulation 5). Only `ps:mccutcheon_cd8` could not be built. The CORUM
   co-complex AUROC of the sources on disk has a median of 0.69 and a range of 0.48–0.99. It was a build check,
   never a filter. High scorers such as the Horlbeck GI maps (0.82–0.83) and `sets:corum` were rarely or never
   kept. (J031–J036, J109; #187.)

**Dataset slate for later work.** The eight datasets of the clean repo's `2.train_test` were all prepared and
passed here: K562, RPE1, HepG2, Nadig Jurkat, KOLF, VCC H1, Shi and Geiger. That module re-processes them with
PIE's pipeline and scores them with PIE's metrics. It does not rerun these arms, so no number below has a PIE-metric
counterpart.

### G002 · External-dataset scorer (`models/ext_score.py`)
- **Question:** Can any arm written against the Marson scorer run unchanged on another dataset with the identical
  panel benchmark? This is session-7 infrastructure that every session-9 run used.
- **Setup:** A standard on-disk format (obs, X_z, X_log2fc, genes, optional replicate halves). A context builder
  mirrors the Marson scorer. A `--drop-sources` flag removes named prior sources (leakage control). Self-test:
  export Marson dev (folds 0–3) to the format and score E000 (Fawkes) and F029 through it.
- **Prediction:** It reproduces the `models/panel/*.json` panel numbers to ≤ 1e-4.
- **Result:** Exact. Max |Δ| = 0 over panel 300 / 100 × Pearson / MSE / retrieval × model / STRING / b.
- **Verdict:** infrastructure; kept.
- **For later work:** Every session-9 score on a non-Marson dataset goes through this scorer, so the numbers are
  directly comparable across datasets. Marson goes through the separate H013x expression route.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/G002-external-runner/` (archived: no), finding #154.

### G003 · Marson's DE preprocessing, reproduced
- **Question:** How were Marson's z and log2FC layers made, and can raw pseudobulk counts reproduce them so that
  external datasets get the same processing?
- **Setup:** A pydeseq2 (0.5.4, separate venv) reimplementation, `de.py`. The design is NB GLM `~ log10(n_cells) +
  donor + target` on guide × donor pseudobulks against all NTC (non-targeting control) pseudobulks, in random
  groups of 50 targets, with no shrinkage. z = log2FC / lfcSE. The filter is n_DE (padj < 0.1) > 75 and pre-QC
  n_cells > 50 ("Marson's rule"). Checked on 100 Marson Stim8hr perturbations from two original GLM groups.
- **Prediction:** Median per-perturbation Pearson ≥ 0.95 for z and for log2FC.
- **Result:**
  - z: median 0.99996 (p10 0.99986).
  - log2FC: median 0.9969 (p10 0.9896).
  - Filter calls: 17 / 17 released passes reproduced, no false pass.
  - n_DE: exact for 67%.
  - Readout-gene selection is not exact (Jaccard 0.855 with the released 10,282 genes).
- **Verdict:** infrastructure; kept.
- **For later work:** Marson's rule, as used by every session-9 dataset without a published knockdown list, is
  this filter. Size factors differ by ~0.15%, so a perturbation within ~5% of 75 DE genes can flip.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/G003-de-pipeline/` (archived: no), finding #154.

### G005 · Song 2025 Jurkat CRISPRi (high MOI), z form
- **Question:** Can Song's genome-wide high-MOI (multiplicity of infection) Jurkat CRISPRi Perturb-seq (374-gene
  readout, rest and anti-TCR + CD28 stimulation) be put into the standard format with Marson-like DE and a
  guide-split r?
- **Setup:**
  - Contrast: target-guide carriers against NT carriers that carry none of the GLM group's targets.
  - Replicates: 4 channel groups as "donors".
  - Control pseudobulks: size-matched (v2, after v1's fixed 95-cell controls mis-calibrated small targets).
  - Filter: ≥ 3 off-target changed genes (75 / 10,282 × 374) and ≥ 50 cells.
  - Halves: guide halves.
- **Prediction:** ≥ 300 Stim targets pass; guide-split r at panel 100 of 0.3–0.6.
- **Result:**
  - Stim: 895 pass (887 exported). Rest: 329 (327 exported).
  - Calibration: olfactory-receptor targets pass 1.6% (Stim).
  - r (z units, fold 0, panel 100): Stim 0.25, Rest 0.06.
- **Verdict:** partly supported. Stim is usable and Rest sits at the noise floor. J104 reuses its cells, guide calls
  and knockdown list in expression space.
- **For later work:** A high-MOI screen needs carrier-vs-carrier contrasts and size-matched controls. Song's Rest
  state is not a usable test set.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/G005-song-jurkat-prep/` (archived: no), findings #153, #156.

### J001-sources · One loader and one pool rule for every source
- **Question:** Can every candidate source, old and new, reach the unchanged pipeline through one loader, so that
  adding a source is one row in `lab/sources.tsv` plus a directory?
- **Setup:** Each source is a gene × feature matrix F with unit rows, and similarity = F[a]·F[b]. Three builders:
  - `profile`: features z-scored, the 3,000 most variable kept, rows unit-normalised, so the dot product is a
    Pearson r.
  - `sets`: sets of 3–500 genes, each weighted by sqrt(ln(N/size)).
  - `graph`: each gene's 20 strongest partners, symmetrised, 0/1 plus the diagonal.

  `pool(dataset, which)` applies rule B, the Marson date rule and the coverage rule. The 41 old names go to the old
  channel code unchanged.
- **Prediction:** The self-check passes, and the K562 current pool equals R002's 40 sources (no `k562`).
- **Result:** The self-check passed. `pool("replogle_k562_expr", "current")` = 40 names and
  `pool("marson_dev", "current")` = 41.
- **Verdict:** infrastructure; kept. The no-change claim is tested by J010.
- **For later work:** Store `release` on every knowledge row (an empty string would pass the date rule) and list
  multi-system Perturb-seq sources as `;`-separated `cell_system`. The one-form design is what the clean repo's
  prior-similarity notebook carries on.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/J001-sources/` (archived: no), finding #186.
