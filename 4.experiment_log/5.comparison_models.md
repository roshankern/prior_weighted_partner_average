# 5 · Comparison models

This stage asks how published perturbation models, and simple variants of the STRING neighbour average, score
against PWPA (Prior-Weighted Partner Average) on the same data, folds and metric. The published models are GEARS,
TxPert and scGPT. The work ran mostly outside the numbered lab sessions: interactive side work on 2026-09-30
(M010–M021, Marson), 2026-10-05 (R001–R011, Replogle K562), 2026-10-06/07 (L001–L006, the "more bars" plan
M012–M030 and R012–R020) and 2026-10-14 (R030). J020 belongs to session 9 (2026-10-05/06) and is the only part with
finding numbers (#189, #196). Datasets: Marson CD4+ T cells (3 states: Rest, Stim8hr, Stim48hr; 2,657 dev rows),
Replogle 2022 K562 essential screen (874 dev knockdowns), and for J020 nine more Perturb-seq datasets.

**Terms used in this file.**
- **k**: the context mean, the mean response of the training knockdowns of that state and fold.
- **b**: the trivial baseline that predicts k for every test knockdown. It scores 0 on Pearson Δ − k by construction.
- **Pearson Δ − k**: Pearson correlation of (prediction − k) with (truth − k) over a gene panel. Higher is better.
- **Panel 300**: a fixed set of 300 readout genes per state × fold, the genes with the largest mean absolute change
  over that fold's training knockdowns. **Panel 100** is the same with 100 genes.
- **MSE**: mean squared error on the same panel, lower is better. Its units depend on the space (below).
- **r**: replicate agreement, the same metric computed between two halves of the data (Marson: donor halves; K562:
  lane halves). A rough ceiling, not a strict roof for MSE.
- **Fawkes** (lab id E000, scorer column `string` or `string_avg`): the STRING neighbour average. STRING v12, top 20
  partners per gene, both directions; a test knockdown is predicted as the plain mean response of its partners that
  are training knockdowns, and as k when it has none. No fitted parameter.
- **dev** = gene folds 0–3; **fold 4** = the **lockbox** (held out; scored once at most).
- **Normalised expression**: pseudobulk samples (Marson: guide × donor; K562: knockdown × lane block) treated as
  cells, log1p of counts per 10,000. Responses are the knockdown mean minus the non-targeting control mean.
- **Decoy**: a copy of a source with gene labels permuted, used as a null in PWPA's L1 selection. **Rule B**: the
  leakage rule that removes a source built from the same cell system (on K562, the `k562` Perturb-seq channel).
- **PWPA** in this stage is the pre-RFE pipeline: H013-plain (one gate per source, L1 path, decoy-calibrated set,
  unpenalised refit). On Marson it is the expression fit H013x-expr-plain; on K562 it is R002-cal.

**Metric in force.** Three scorers touch this stage. Do not compare numbers across them.
1. Old metric (sessions 1–4, `lab/score.py`): Pearson Δ − k on each test row's own top-N genes, in z-score /
   log2FC space. Biased: it rewards any prediction along k ("k × 1.1" scored +0.29). Only M010–M021's first scores.
2. Session-5 fixed panel in z-score space (`models/panel_score.py`, `models/RESULTS.md`, 2026-09-30): panel 300,
   MSE in z units (b ≈ 0.117).
3. Fixed panel 300 on normalised expression (`models/ext_score.py` for K562 and session-9 datasets;
   `models/more_bars/score_marson.py` and `lab/exp/H013x-expr-plain/score_expr.py` for Marson). MSE in
   log1p-expression units (Marson b 0.0181, K562 b 0.0349). This is the scorer of record for everything here from
   R001 on, and for the Marson rescoring of M010 and M020.

## What this stage established

1. **Every published deep model scored below the zero-parameter Fawkes on Pearson Δ − k on Marson and K562, and far
   below PWPA.** Normalised expression, panel 300, dev:
   - Marson (Fawkes 0.1661 ± 0.0068, PWPA 0.2706 ± 0.0076): GEARS + STRING 0.0466 ± 0.0076, GEARS + GO
     0.0413 ± 0.0075, GEARS + GO + STRING 0.0496 ± 0.0078, TxPert + STRING 0.0772 ± 0.0087, TxPert + STRING + GO
     0.0770 ± 0.0088, scGPT 0.0017 ± 0.0035 (M010–M030).
   - K562 (Fawkes 0.5210 ± 0.0114, PWPA 0.6000 ± 0.0104): GEARS + STRING 0.4333 ± 0.0110, GEARS + GO 0.3684 ± 0.0122,
     GEARS + GO + STRING 0.4251 ± 0.0113, TxPert + STRING 0.4700 ± 0.0115, TxPert + STRING + GO 0.4729 ± 0.0113,
     scGPT 0.0004 ± 0.0055 (R010–R020).
   - Paired to Fawkes, the best published bar loses by −0.0890 ± 0.0096 on Marson (TxPert + STRING + GO) and
     −0.0482 ± 0.0071 on K562. The Marson fold-4 scores say the same (TxPert + STRING 0.0950, Fawkes 0.1986).
2. **Giving the published models their papers' priors (GO, or GO + STRING) changed nothing measurable.** GEARS + GO −
   GEARS + STRING: Marson dev −0.0053 ± 0.0095, K562 −0.0649 ± 0.0109 (GO worse). TxPert + STRING + GO − TxPert +
   STRING: Marson dev −0.0002 ± 0.0072, K562 +0.0029 ± 0.0052. GEARS + GO + STRING − GEARS + STRING: Marson dev
   +0.0029 ± 0.0078, K562 −0.0081 ± 0.0080 (M012, M013, M022, R012–R014).
3. **Where published models score at or below b.** scGPT is at b on Pearson on both datasets (Marson +0.0015 ± 0.0032
   paired to b; K562 +0.0004 ± 0.0055) and worse than b on MSE (Marson 0.0196 vs 0.0181; K562 0.0363 vs 0.0349).
   On Marson every GEARS and TxPert bar is worse than b on MSE (0.0199–0.0210 vs 0.0181), and so is Fawkes (0.0221);
   only PWPA (0.0154) and PWPA on STRING alone (0.0169, L003) beat b there. In J020, TxPert + STRING is at or below b
   on Pearson on Song (−0.005, panel 100) and VCC H1 (−0.040), and at or above b on MSE on Song, KOLF, Shi, Geiger
   and VCC H1 (#189, #196). On K562 every GEARS and TxPert bar beats b on MSE.
4. **Across ten datasets TxPert + STRING beats Fawkes only on Nadig HepG2 (+0.050 ± 0.010), and PWPA (41 sources)
   beats both on all ten** (J020, #189, #196, session-9 summary). TxPert is level on RPE1 (+0.003 ± 0.013) and Nadig
   Jurkat (−0.013 ± 0.006), and loses on K562 (−0.048 ± 0.007) and on every dataset under 710 knockdowns (Song
   −0.099, KOLF −0.126, Shi −0.091, Geiger −0.145, VCC H1 −0.194). One seed each.
5. **The graph carries signal inside GEARS and TxPert, but they use it worse than averaging.** STRING − randomized
   STRING inside each model (Marson, old metric, dev): GEARS +0.056 ± 0.009, TxPert +0.062 ± 0.010. That is about a
   quarter of what STRING − randomized gives the neighbour average (+0.20 to +0.27) (M010, M011, M020, M021).
6. **Fawkes variants without learned source weights do not close the gap to PWPA.** Best single other source as the
   neighbour list: Marson depmap 0.1937 (+0.0277 ± 0.0077 vs Fawkes), coess 0.1805 (+0.0144 ± 0.0073); on K562 no
   source beats STRING (depmap −0.0739 ± 0.0101) (L001). Pooling PWPA's 41 sources with fixed weights gives
   −0.0347 ± 0.0071 / −0.1794 ± 0.0102 (equal per source, Marson / K562) or +0.0069 ± 0.0029 / +0.0115 ± 0.0023
   (equal per class); PWPA stays +0.0976 ± 0.0073 / +0.0675 ± 0.0054 above the per-class run (L002). Fitting one
   weight per training knockdown on STRING gives +0.018 ± 0.003 on K562 and −0.001 ± 0.001 on Marson (L004, L006).
7. **PWPA restricted to STRING is Fawkes, rescaled.** With one 0/1 channel the entmax weights reduce to
   P − k = c (Fawkes − k) with c > 0, so Pearson Δ − k equals Fawkes row by row: Marson 0.1653 ± 0.0069 vs 0.1661
   (paired −0.0008 ± 0.0005, all of it from the 284 rows with no trained partner), K562 0.5210 vs 0.5210. Only MSE
   moves: Marson 0.0169 vs 0.0221 (−0.0052 ± 0.0003; below b by −0.0011 ± 0.0002), K562 0.0234 vs 0.0239
   (−0.0005 ± 0.0001). So PWPA's Pearson lead over Fawkes comes from the other sources, and part of its MSE lead from
   this shrinkage (L003).
8. **On K562 the 15-dataset PWPA pool beats the 41-source pool by +0.0131 ± 0.0023** (0.6131 vs 0.6000; MSE
   −0.0010 ± 0.0002), with the old L1 + decoy selection; superseded by RFE (R030).
9. **Likely cause of the deep-model deficit: mostly real for this regime, with two known setup weaknesses.** The
   ranking held under three metrics, two datasets, dev and fold 4, several graphs and ten datasets, and nothing was
   tuned against the comparators. But the models were trained on a few thousand pseudobulk samples (Marson ≈ 7.2k per
   TxPert model, K562 ≈ 5.2k) instead of the ~160k single cells of their papers, with one seed and paper defaults.
   Two cases are clear setup failures and are flagged as such: GEARS with library defaults (fixed, see M010) and
   scGPT's flag-on-input-token design, which cannot represent a knockdown of a non-readout gene (525 of 874 K562 test
   rows). Neither explains scGPT's zero on the rows it did predict (0.010 ± 0.007 on 349 K562 rows).

## Experiments

### M010 · GEARS + STRING on Marson
- **Question:** Does a published graph model (GEARS) beat the STRING neighbour average when given the same STRING
  graph and the same Marson data?
- **Setup:** Marson pseudobulk (guide × donor samples as cells), 3 states, dev folds 0–3 and fold 4. Stock GEARS
  (`cell-gears` 0.1.2, torch 2.6.0, torch_geometric 2.8), GO graph replaced by STRING v12 top-20 (both directions,
  weight 1; 348,132 directed edges); GEARS's own co-expression graph from training samples. Pert nodes = the full
  11,526-gene universe. Defaults: hidden 64, one GNN layer each, decoder hidden 16, 20 epochs, Adam lr 1e-3 with
  StepLR × 0.5 per epoch, batch 32, best epoch by val top-20 DE MSE on a 10% gene split. **One change: weight decay
  5e-4 → 0**, chosen on dev. Prediction made once at the control mean (stock `predict()` averages 300 random controls,
  which adds noise up to 0.12 log1p). 15 models (3 states × 4 dev folds + fold 4), one seed. Fallback to k for the 2
  dev knockdowns with no STRING edge (SACM1L).
- **Prediction:** Not recorded as a number. The contract (`models/CONTRACT.md`) framed it as a comparison to E000.
- **Result:**
  - With full defaults (v0) GEARS scored Pearson Δ − k −0.197 (old metric, panel N = 300, dev), MSE 0.778 (b 0.685).
    A diagnostic showed it predicted only 10–30% of the shared knockdown response even on its own training
    knockdowns (β 0.32, observed 1.07). Cause: the (P − y)^4 loss gives tiny gradients on pseudobulk log1p, and
    Adam's L2 weight decay swamps them. With weight decay 0, val β ≈ 0.96.
  - Old metric (own top-300, z / log2FC space): dev 0.058 vs E000 0.215 (paired −0.157 ± 0.009); fold 4 0.095 vs
    0.229 (−0.134 ± 0.018). MSE log2FC dev 0.620 vs b 0.685 and E000 0.659.
  - Session-5 fixed panel (z space): dev 0.036 (−0.147 ± 0.009), fold 4 0.056 (−0.144 ± 0.018); MSE 0.141 dev vs
    b 0.117.
  - Normalised expression, panel 300: dev 0.0466 ± 0.0076, MSE 0.0208 ± 0.0004 (Fawkes 0.1661 / 0.0221, b 0.0002 /
    0.0181, PWPA 0.2706 / 0.0154). By state: Rest −0.0193, Stim8hr 0.0475, Stim48hr 0.1116. Fold 4 0.0599 / 0.0208.
    PWPA − GEARS + STRING (paired, dev): +0.2240 ± 0.0101.
  - Transfer: the deficit to Fawkes held under all three metrics. The old "GEARS beats b on MSE" reversed under the
    panel metrics (worse than b in z space and in expression). In expression GEARS still beats Fawkes on MSE.
- **Verdict:** baseline (comparator bar). Kept as the GEARS + STRING bar of the score figure.
- **For later work:** GEARS defaults fail silently on pseudobulk: check that a model predicts the shared response on
  its own training knockdowns before scoring it. Predict at the control mean, not with stock `predict()`. GEARS beats
  Fawkes on MSE by being smooth and near k, not by knockdown-specific signal (Rest state is below b on Pearson).
- **Source:** `lab/exp/M010-gears-string/` (archived: yes, no README; `arm.py` and `metrics*.json` only),
  `models/gears/README.md`, `models/RESULTS.md`, `models/h013x_expr_score.json`. No finding number.

### M020 · TxPert + STRING on Marson
- **Question:** Does TxPert (Exphormer graph transformer) beat Fawkes with the same STRING graph on Marson?
- **Setup:** TxPert from the txpert-baseline checkout (package `gspp`; version not recorded), model block of

  samples per model, about 5.6k steps (vs about 125k on the paper's K562). Prediction averaged over the 4 donor
  basal states. 4 fallbacks to k (SACM1L, AMD1: no STRING edge). 150 epochs were piloted on dev fold 0 and rejected.
  12–17 min per model on one L40S, 15 models.
- **Prediction:** Not recorded.
- **Result:**
  - Old metric (own top-300): dev 0.109 vs E000 0.215 (paired −0.107 ± 0.010); fold 4 0.127 (−0.102 ± 0.019); MSE
    0.693 vs b 0.685.
  - Session-5 fixed panel (z space): dev 0.075 (−0.108 ± 0.009), fold 4 0.096 (−0.104 ± 0.018); MSE 0.125 vs b 0.117.

    0.0950, Stim48hr 0.1442. Fold 4 0.0950 ± 0.0162, MSE 0.0196 ± 0.0008. Fawkes 0.1661 (fold 4 0.1986).
  - Transfer: the deficit to Fawkes (about −0.10) held under all three metrics. MSE worse than b under all three.
- **Verdict:** baseline. Kept as the TxPert + STRING bar.
- **For later work:** On Marson TxPert reaches about half the neighbour average. Anchoring matters: an unanchored
  quick read gave 0.18 on Stim8hr fold 0 against the scorer's 0.117 (old metric); always score through the shared
  adapter.

### M030 · scGPT on Marson
- **Question:** Does a pretrained foundation model (scGPT, whole-human checkpoint) beat Fawkes after fine-tuning?
- **Setup:** scGPT 0.2.5 (GitHub main, commit `cebd6fa`), torch 2.3.1, flash-attn 2.6.3, the authors' perturbation
  tutorial unchanged (`TransformerGenerator`, pretrained encoder weights, lr 1e-4, batch 64, up to 15 epochs, early
  stop on 10% of training knockdowns). Gene tokens from the authors' ENSG table (0 of 10,273 readouts missing).
  All 10,273 readouts are input genes; 1,536 drawn per training batch. Prediction = mean model output over 300
  control samples. A test knockdown whose gene is not a readout cannot be flagged and gets the mean training knockdown
  (256 of 2,657 dev). About 40 min per model on an L40S, 15 models, one seed. One fix was allowed if the model
  predicted under half the shared response (the smoke fold gave 1.19, so none was made).
- **Prediction:** Not recorded as a number.
- **Result:** Normalised expression, panel 300, dev: Pearson Δ − k 0.0017 ± 0.0035, MSE 0.0196 ± 0.0005. Minus b
  +0.0015 ± 0.0032 / +0.0015 ± 0.0000; minus Fawkes −0.1643 ± 0.0076 / −0.0026 ± 0.0004; minus TxPert + STRING
  −0.0755 ± 0.0092. By state 0.0086 / 0.0037 / −0.0071. **Fold 4: not scored.** The three fold-4 prediction files
  exist, but no score file was written and the README still has placeholders.
- **Verdict:** baseline; at b on Pearson, worse than b on MSE.
- **For later work:** The model predicts nothing knockdown-specific, even on its training knockdowns (median
  correlation after removing the shared response −0.01 to 0.07). It adds a generic shift 3–4 times larger than the
  true shared response, unstable across states (share 0.06–0.64 at Rest, 2.12–2.54 at Stim48hr). An untested likely
  cause: trained on 1,536 genes per batch, predicted on 10,273. Setup or real: partly setup (that train/predict
  mismatch is in the tutorial), but a level fix would at best bring MSE to b and leave Pearson at 0.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/M030-scgpt/` (archived: no), `models/scgpt/RUN.md`.

### R001 · Replogle K562 in the external-scorer format (prep)
