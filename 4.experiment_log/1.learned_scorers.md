# 1 · Learned scorers

This stage asked whether a model learned by gradient descent can replace the hand-built partner-weighting rules of
sessions 1–4 (E119, E121), and how simple that learned model can be. It covers the F series, F001–F035, and the
session-7 transfer tests (G001–G007) that ran the frozen F models on other T-cell data. F001–F007 ran on 2026-09-30
(a "flexible-models" plan the researcher asked for after session 4). F008–F015 ran in session 5 (2026-10-01), after
the metric fix. F016–F033 ran in session 6 (2026-10-01 22:52 to 2026-10-02 07:50 EDT). G001–G007 ran in session 7
(from 2026-10-02 08:30; results harvested 2026-10-04). F034–F035 ran outside the loop on 2026-10-03/04 and were
logged in session 8. Data: Marson CD4+ T-cell CRISPR knockdowns in three conditions (Rest, Stim8hr, Stim48hr), split
into 5 gene folds. Every metric is computed per condition, then averaged. The metric changed inside this stage:
F001–F007 were scored on the old biased metric; from F008 on, everything uses the session-5 fixed panel.

**Terms used in this file.**
- **Δ_j**: the measured response (one value per readout gene) of training knockdown j. **k**: the context mean, i.e.
  the fold's mean training Δ in that condition. **b**: the baseline "predict k for every knockdown".
- **z / log2FC**: the two layers of the truth. Direction is scored in z (z = log2FC / its standard error), size in log2FC.
- **Old metric (sessions 1–4, F001–F007 here):** Pearson Δ − k and MSE on each test row's own top-N genes by |z|
  (N = 300 primary, N = 100 also reported). It is biased: genes picked for being extreme in the truth overshoot k, so
  "k × 1.1" scored +0.29, above STRING. About two thirds of E119's lead over STRING was this artifact. Its MSE values
  (b 0.685 at N = 300) are on a different scale from the panel MSE.
- **Panel metric (from session 5):** one fixed panel per (condition, fold) of the 300 genes (**panel 300**, primary) or
  100 genes (**panel 100**, must hold) with the largest mean |z| over that fold's *training* knockdowns. Direction =
  Pearson Δ − k on the panel (b = 0; k × 1.1 = −0.02). Size = raw log2FC MSE on the panel (b = 0.117 dev, 0.1147
  lockbox; lower is better). **Retrieval** (diagnostic): rank of a row's own truth among all rows by similarity to the
  prediction; 0.5 is chance.
- **r**: the donor-split roof (one donor half predicting the other), 0.397 dev panel 300 for Pearson. MSE r (0.211) is
  not a roof.
- **dev** = folds 0–3; **lockbox** = fold 4, scored once at a session's end.
- **STRING (E000)**: the zero-parameter average of a target's STRING-network neighbours' responses (the
  knowledge-graph baseline). Dev panel 300: 0.183 / MSE 0.144; lockbox 0.200 / 0.1302.
- **Datasets (priors)**: **D** DepMap (co-essentiality "co-ess" E043 and raw gene effects E089b count as one),
  **K** Replogle K562 Perturb-seq similarity (E045; "own-K562" = the target's own K562 profile, E057), **T** 37 T-cell
  CRISPR screens (E109d), **L** LLM programme labels (E008; pair = same primary label), **S** STRING v12 top-20
  adjacency. "All 5" = D K T L S.
- **perm0**: a control that permutes each prior source's gene → row map within its coverage, so gene identity is gone
  but the coverage pattern (which genes have a row; the "present flags") is kept. **perm0all** also permutes the flags.
- **The bar** (session-6 rule): an extra dataset or hand-built component must buy about +0.03 panel Pearson with MSE no
  worse; inside the bar the simpler or more learned model is the front point.
- **entmax-1.5**: a sparse softmax; weights can be exactly 0.
- Older reference arms: **E071a** zero-parameter neighbour average (panel 0.234 / 0.122), **E119** hand-fitted blend
  (old metric 0.387 / 0.532; panel 0.231 / 0.230), **E121** E119 with its NNLS weights refit on the training panel
  (panel 0.294 / 0.108), **E110e** sized kernel (old metric 0.364 / 0.577).

## What this stage established

1. **The session-5 metric fix reversed the old flexible winner, and every training loss must select genes like the
   panel.** F004 (MLP → 64-PC response basis) scored 0.396 Pearson on the old metric (+0.181 ± 0.008 vs STRING, "ties
   E119"). On the fixed panel the same predictions score 0.170 (−0.014 ± 0.009 vs STRING) with MSE 0.2645 against
   b 0.117. Retrained with the panel loss (F008) it scores 0.257 / 0.111. The old-metric lead of F001 (+0.149 ± 0.007
   vs STRING) shrank to +0.115 ± 0.008 when retrained on the panel (F009). (F004, F008, F009; #129, #130.)
2. **Attention over the training knockdowns ("memory") is the core of every good model.** On the panel, the
   memory-only learned multi-kernel F009 scores 0.298 / 0.105. A parametric head alone (F008) scores 0.257, learned
   per-source encoders (F010) 0.245 with MSE above b (0.119). Inside F013c's hybrid, removing the memory path costs
   −0.064 ± 0.004. (F009, F008, F010, F025c; #132, #143.)
3. **A tiny MLP that scores each (target, partner) pair from the prior similarities, with entmax-1.5 attention,
   matches or beats every hand-shaped kernel and the hybrid.** F026 (all 5 datasets, ~1.6k parameters): 0.311 /
   0.1034, −0.007 ± 0.004 Pearson and −0.0018 ± 0.0006 MSE vs F013c. F029 (DepMap only, 1,348 parameters): 0.278 /
   0.1078, +0.013 ± 0.005 over F013c's hybrid on the same data. The attention it learns is broad (~160–250 of ~660
   partners), not sparse; sparser attention raised the inner validation loss. (F018, F026, F029; #141, #148, #149.)
4. **The gains come from gene identity in the priors (perm0 controls).** Panel 300 Pearson: F013c-perm0 0.031,
   F018-perm0 0.089 (F018 − perm0 +0.197 ± 0.008), F026-perm0 0.087 (+0.225 ± 0.009), F029-perm0 0.063 (+0.215 ±
   0.010). All perm0 MSEs sit at b (0.116–0.118). The 0.06–0.09 left in the MLP perm0s comes from the kept coverage
   pattern. On the old metric the controls looked far stronger (F001-perm0 0.224, F004-perm0all 0.290), another sign
   of its bias. (#131, #141, #148, #149.)
5. **DepMap is the core prior; each extra dataset adds about +0.01, and none clears the bar alone.** Inside the pair
   scorer: D 0.278 → D+S 0.289 (+0.011 ± 0.004) ≈ D+K 0.287 → D+K+S 0.2995 (+0.013 ± 0.004) → all 5 0.311 (+0.012 ±
   0.004). In F013c the three extras (T, L, S) add −0.002 / +0.007 / +0.007 (each ± 0.005) alone but +0.030 ± 0.005
   together. STRING substitutes for K562. New cell-line Perturb-seq (X-Atlas, +0.001 ± 0.002), a learned DepMap → K562
   metric (+0.006 ± 0.003) and CD4 expression atlases (−0.002) add < 0.01. (F016, F017, F019, F022, F024, F033;
   #135, #136, #138, #140, #142, #151.)
6. **The extras pay on stimulated, TCR-proximal targets that have no K562 profile.** tcr_signalling targets (37 rows,
   2.1%) carry 20.7% of the stimulated-state Pearson gain of 5-dataset over 2-dataset models and 96% of the MSE gain.
   The D+K models predict CD247, LAT, CD3D, LCP2 and LCK with the wrong sign (Pearson −0.3 to −0.5); the 5-dataset
   models reach 0.7–0.98. Rows without a K562 profile (48%) carry 67% of the gain. (F027; #145.)
7. **Size (MSE) moves only through direction; extra size, strength or per-condition machinery adds nothing.** With
   one size per condition, MSE skill 1 − MSE/b ≈ (mean Pearson)² (#134). A per-row size head from partner agreement
   (F021) moves MSE by −0.0001 ± 0.0001. A partner-strength logit term (F020) +0.004 ± 0.003 Pearson. Per-condition
   source weights (F030) +0.0008 ± 0.0010. Separate per-state fits (F035a–c) tie the joint fit (−0.006 ± 0.006,
   −0.006 ± 0.003, −0.000 ± 0.003). One attention graph serves both z and log2FC (F034: MSE +0.0002 ± 0.0001).
   (#134, #137, #139, #147, #157, #158.)
8. **Averaging two learned families beats either alone; it is family diversity, not ensemble size.** ½(F013c + F009)
   = F023, 0.326 / 0.1040, ties the half-hand-built F015 (−0.004 ± 0.004). F013c with 10 seeds = 5 seeds (−0.0005 ±
   0.0007), and F023 − F031 = +0.0085 ± 0.0032. ½(F013c + F026) = F032, 0.329 / 0.1030, is the best dev point on both
   axes. (F015, F023, F031, F032; #133, #144, #150.)
9. **The lockbox replicates the dev order.** Fold 4, panel 300: F032 0.321 (+0.121 ± 0.015 vs STRING) / 0.0927;
   F026 0.315 (+0.115 ± 0.015) / 0.0929; F029 0.266 (+0.066 ± 0.016) / 0.1035; F034 (session 8) 0.315 / 0.0934.
   F026 − F029 on fold 4: +0.049 ± 0.010 (dev +0.033). (#152; session-8 lockbox.)
10. **The 5-dataset models transfer to two Jurkat datasets with frozen weights; the DepMap-only model shrinks.**
    Nadig resting Jurkat (K562 dropped, same lab): F026 +0.103 ± 0.008 over STRING, F029 +0.035 ± 0.010. Song
    stimulated Jurkat (panel 100): F026 +0.114 ± 0.009, F029 +0.028 ± 0.010. Marson's order (F032 ≈ F026 > F029 >
    STRING) held on both. No usable primary-T-cell test set existed. (G001, G006, G007; #153, #155, #156.)

## Experiments

### F001 · Learned multi-kernel attention
- **Question:** Can one softmax with learned source weights recover E119's hand choices (source weights, r³
  sharpness, missing-source handling, size)? First direction of the 2026-09-30 flexible-models plan.
- **Setup:** Marson dev (folds 0–3), old metric (own top-N, N = 300 and 100). P_t = k_c + s_c·(Σ_j a_tj Δ_j − k_c), a =
  softmax over condition c's training knockdowns; logits = Σ_m w_m·sign(r_m)|r_m|^{p_m} (a learned bias μ_m when a
  source is missing) + β·log‖D_j‖. Six sources: co-ess, K562, raw DepMap, T-cell screens, LLM label, STRING top-20.
  The README says 20 parameters; the session-6 count for this form is 22 (w, p, μ × 6, β, s × 3). Full-batch Adam on
  the top-300 |z| MSE of leave-one-out training targets, 25% gene-level inner early stopping, 5-seed ensemble, z and
  log2FC fit separately. Comparators E119, E110e, STRING. Control F001-perm0.
- **Prediction:** Pearson ≥ E110e (0.364) and within 0.02 of E119 (≥ 0.367) at N = 300; MSE ≤ 0.577.
- **Result:** Old metric, dev, N = 300: Pearson 0.363 (Rest 0.327 / Stim8hr 0.367 / Stim48hr 0.396), +0.149 ±
  0.007 vs STRING; vs E119 −0.024 ± 0.005; vs E110e −0.001 ± 0.005. MSE 0.533 (b 0.685, STRING 0.659); vs E119 +0.001
  ± 0.003; vs E110e −0.045 ± 0.004. N = 100: 0.374 / 0.750. perm0: 0.224 / 0.572 (F001 − perm0 +0.139). Learned: w ≈
  6–9 for co-ess, K562, DepMap; sharpness p 0.3–0.9 (flatter than r³) except T-cell 1.3–2.2; β 2–3.4; s ≈ 1.1–1.3.
  Not rescored as is on the panel. Retrained with the panel loss as F009: panel 300 0.298 (+0.115 ± 0.008 vs
  STRING). The lead over STRING transferred but shrank.
- **Verdict:** superseded by F009 (same model, unbiased loss). Partly supported at the time: tied E110e, missed E119.
- **For later work:** a learned per-source weight over prior similarities is the seed of every later model. Its
  old-metric numbers are inflated; use F009's.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/F001-learned-multikernel/` (archived: no; F001-perm0 archived: yes), docs/plans/2026-09-30-flexible-models.md; findings #132 (via F009).

### F002 · Per-source learned encoders with attention
- **Question:** Do learned per-source embeddings (PCA → MLP → 32-d, pooled by learned gates) find better phenocopy
  partners than F001's per-source correlation r?
- **Setup:** Old metric, dev. Per source: top 64 PCs of the outside matrix (label: 41-d one-hot) → MLP 64 → 64 → 32;
  attention by dot product / τ + β·log‖D_j‖ + w·STRING. ~20k parameters, one model per condition, AdamW lr 0.01,
  600 steps, source dropout 0.2, 5 seeds. Control F002-perm0.
- **Prediction:** between E071a (0.306) and E119 (0.387), within ± 0.02 of F001.
- **Result:** Old metric, dev, N = 300: Pearson 0.310 (0.269 / 0.324 / 0.337), +0.095 ± 0.009 vs STRING, −0.053 ±
  0.008 vs F001, −0.077 ± 0.008 vs E119. MSE 0.514 (vs E119 −0.018 ± 0.004). perm0 0.101 / 0.584. Early stopping
  at median step 62–70 of 600; gates stayed near the uniform init. Panel retrain (F010): 0.2449 (+0.0615 ± 0.0092
  vs STRING) / MSE 0.1191, above b 0.1173. The Pearson loss to F001/F009 transferred; the old MSE edge reversed.
- **Verdict:** failed. Learned encoders overfit in ~65 steps with ~660 training genes per condition.
- **For later work:** do not learn gene embeddings from the priors with this little data; use the given
  similarities as inputs.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/F002-source-encoder-attention/` (archived: yes); findings #132 (via F010).

### F004 · Parametric regression to a response basis
- **Question:** Can a model that stores the response geometry in its weights (no memory lookup) match neighbour
  lookup (E119, F001)?
- **Setup:** Old metric, dev. V_c = top 64 right singular vectors of the training D = Δ − k_c. Inputs: each source
  (co-ess, K562, DepMap, T-cell, label) projected on its own top 64 PCs, plus 5 present flags. MLP 325 → 256 → 256
  (GELU, dropout 0.2) + 3 condition heads → 64 coefficients; P = k_c + V_c·h_c. Loss: MSE on each training gene's own
  top-300 |z| genes. AdamW lr 1e-3, 20% source dropout, 5 seeds. Controls perm0, perm0all, flagsonly.
- **Prediction:** below E119 and F001, about 0.30–0.35; MSE between E119 (0.532) and STRING (0.659).
- **Result:** Old metric, dev, N = 300: Pearson 0.396 (0.339 / 0.421 / 0.429), +0.181 ± 0.008 vs STRING; vs E119
  +0.009 ± 0.006; vs F001 +0.033 ± 0.007. MSE 0.400 (b 0.685); vs E119 −0.132 ± 0.005. N = 100: 0.409 / 0.511.
  Controls (old metric): perm0 0.292 / 0.437; perm0all 0.290 / 0.445; flagsonly 0.316 / 0.418. F004 − perm0all
  +0.106 ± 0.007 Pearson. A feature-free model already beat E119's MSE by 0.087 ± 0.006.
  **Session-5 rescoring (panel 300, dev, same predictions):** Pearson 0.170 (−0.014 ± 0.009 vs STRING), MSE 0.2645
  (b 0.1173); perm0all −0.024 / 0.302. The conclusion reversed: F004 fell below STRING and far above b on MSE,
  because its loss used truth-picked genes.
- **Verdict:** failed under the corrected metric; superseded by F008 (panel loss) and then by memory models.
- **For later work:** never train on each row's own top-N genes. A parametric head alone, even with the fair loss
  (F008, 0.257), trails memory models.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/F004-basis-regression/` (archived: no; perm0 / perm0all / flagsonly archived: yes); findings #129, #130.

### F013c · Learned hybrid: PC head + memory + own-K562 (and F013, F013b, F013d)
- **Question:** One flexible model fed everything E121 uses, learning kernel, source weights, gating and size itself.
- **Setup:** Panel metric, dev, all 5 datasets. Per condition: V_c = top 64 PCs of training D. coeffs_t =
  head_c(h_t) (MLP trunk 256 hidden on all source PCs + flags) + γ_c(h_t)·Σ_j softmax_j(ℓ_tj) C_j (memory) +
  β_c(h_t)·own-K562 profile on V_c; ℓ_tj = Σ_m (a_m r_m + b_m r_m³)·present_m + a_s·STRING. Panel loss, 25% gene
  inner early stopping, source dropout, 5 seeds. F013 = with a learned query·key term and one lr 1e-3; F013b = no
  query·key; F013c = F013b with kernel lr 0.05 and nets lr 1e-4; F013d = F013c plus query·key. Control F013c-perm0.
- **Prediction:** none recorded (no README).
- **Result:** F013 and F013b: every seed early-stopped at step 10 with the kernel weights still at init; not
  scored on the panel. F013c, panel 300, dev: Pearson 0.318 (+0.1346 ± 0.0079 vs STRING; 0.264 / 0.317 / 0.373),
  MSE 0.1052; panel 100 0.336 / 0.0757. vs E121 +0.024 Pearson / −0.003 MSE (unpaired, under the bar). F013d
  0.3197 / 0.1051 (no effect). perm0 0.031 / 0.118. Learned a (r weight): co-ess 4.63, K562 2.96, DepMap 4.78,
  T-cell 2.86, label and STRING ~0.9 each. Lockbox (fold 4, scored by the researcher after session 5): 0.2951
  (+0.0948 ± 0.0157 vs STRING) / 0.0952 (b 0.1147).
- **Verdict:** kept as the 5-dataset front point at the start of session 6; superseded as single model by F026
  (simpler, tied); used as one half of F023 and F032.
- **For later work:** give the kernel weights their own, higher learning rate, or the net early-stops before they
  move. A learned query·key term adds nothing. Its head and memory need each other (F025).
- **Source:** `lab/exp/F013c-hybrid-kernel-lr/` (archived: yes; F013, F013b, F013d archived: yes); findings #131, #132.

### F018 · MLP pair scorer with entmax attention, D+K
- **Question:** Can a small MLP that scores each (target, partner) pair from raw source similarities replace F009's
  hand-shaped kernel, with sparse attention keeping a few partners?
- **Setup:** Panel metric, dev, D+K. P_t = k_c + s_c·Σ_j a_tj D_j; a = entmax-1.5 over MLP(r_coess, r_depmap, r_k562,
  3 present flags, condition one-hot), 9 → 32 → 32 → 1 GELU, ≈ 1.4k parameters, last layer zero-initialised (starts
  at uniform ≈ b). r divided by its std over present training pairs. Learned size per condition, AdamW lr 3e-3, panel
  loss, 5 seeds, z and log2FC fit separately. Comparators F016d, F009, F013c. Control F018-perm0.
- **Prediction:** ≥ F016d + 0.01 Pearson with MSE ≤ F016d's; within −0.01 of F009; ≲ 20 partners per target.
- **Result:** Panel 300, dev: Pearson 0.287 (+0.103 ± 0.008 vs STRING; 0.262 / 0.269 / 0.329); vs F016d +0.0083 ±
  0.0040, vs F009 −0.0115 ± 0.0051, vs F013c −0.0314 ± 0.0057 (Rest −0.002, Stim8hr −0.048, Stim48hr −0.044). MSE
  0.107 (vs F016d −0.0003 ± 0.0002; vs b −0.0099 ± 0.0006). Panel 100 0.305 / 0.079, the same differences. Partners
  with nonzero weight 193 / 223 / 205 per condition, top-1 weight 0.11–0.12; sparser attention later in training
  raised the inner validation loss.
- **Verdict:** kept: the more learned D+K point at equal score, replacing F016d. The sparsity hypothesis failed.
- **For later work:** the scorer family (F026, F029, F034) starts here. Do not force sparse attention; entmax-1.5
  stays broad at the chosen checkpoints.
- **Source:** `lab/exp/F018-sparse-mlp-attention/` (archived: no); findings #141.

### F018-perm0 · F018 with permuted priors
- **Question:** Control: does F018's gain come from the DepMap and K562 similarities?
- **Setup:** F018 unchanged with co-ess, DepMap and K562 gene → row maps permuted; present flags kept.
- **Prediction:** Pearson ≤ F018 − 0.15, MSE near b.
- **Result:** Panel 300: 0.089 (−0.094 ± 0.010 vs STRING) / 0.116 (b 0.117); retrieval 0.553. Paired vs F018:
  −0.197 ± 0.008 Pearson (panel 100 −0.207 ± 0.009), +0.0089 ± 0.0005 MSE. Attention went flat (≈ 415 partners,
